## Research objective

The goal is not to produce better market stories.

The goal is to learn which observable sequences change decision quality, in which regimes, with what false-positive and false-negative cost.

Important distinctions include:

```text
BTC health != ecosystem health
liquidity existence != liquidity deployment
ETF inflow != alt rotation confirmation
stablecoin growth != deployment confirmation
single spike != persistence
BTC permission != alt permission
```

## High-value mission seeds

### 1. Sequence memory

Test whether ordered transitions and persistence add value beyond snapshot indicators.

### 2. False-negative accountability

Measure opportunity cost from delayed deployment, excessive confirmation requirements and uniformly defensive rules. Do not weaken safety based on anecdotes; quantify the trade-off.

### 3. BTC vs alt offensive profiles

Test whether different asset classes/horizons need different permission profiles rather than one universal conservatism level.

### 4. ETF-era transmission

Separate institutional BTC demand, ecosystem liquidity, ETH leadership, breadth and actual capital deployment. Challenge assumptions inherited from pre-ETF cycles.

### 5. Failed-move / survival analysis

Study what distinguishes durable recovery/rotation from mechanical bounce, fake rotation or liquidity mirage.

## Scientific discipline

Historical evidence can suggest hypotheses. It must not silently rewrite prospective forecasts or promote itself.

Separate:

```text
descriptive finding
mechanism hypothesis
historical replay
forward-test candidate
validated prospective edge
canonical decision rule
```

Those are different states.

## Authority ceiling

Default mode is `READ_ONLY_RESEARCH`.

Do not create new thresholds, weights, portfolio actions or canonical rules during exploratory analysis. New market concepts should face a complexity tax and kill criteria before admission.

Strong contradictory evidence is valuable even when it weakens a favorite framework belief.

## Astra-class challenge

A stronger model should try to find where the framework's safety culture became too uniform.

The best result may be:

- confirm the current defensive posture;
- prove it should vary by asset/horizon;
- identify a simpler sequence variable that subsumes several indicators;
- show that an apparent edge disappears against a mechanical baseline;
- show that opportunity-cost claims are not reproducible.

Do not optimize for a bullish or bearish answer.
