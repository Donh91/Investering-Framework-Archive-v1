## 1. COLDCARD incident

Classification: `SECURITY_INCIDENT_CONTEXT_HIGH_USER_PROTECTION_LOW_IMMEDIATE_MARKET_STATE_RELEVANCE`.

The incident is real, official and operationally serious for affected users. It belongs in the security/event archive because compromised seed generation can create abnormal on-chain transfers and self-custody risk narratives.

It must not be used as an explanation for BTC price, ETF flow, rotation or broad liquidity without a time-aligned on-chain flow study showing material market-scale effects. Repeated official posts are one evolving incident, not independent catalyst counts.

The Chainalysis scale claim remains quarantined until a direct report, reproducible address ledger or equivalent primary evidence is available.

## 2. Circle / Arc / Circle National Trust

Classification: `STRUCTURAL_STABLECOIN_AND_INSTITUTIONAL_SETTLEMENT_EVENT`.

This is the highest-value candidate in the batch because it may affect several framework layers over months rather than minutes:

- stablecoin regulatory credibility and custody architecture;
- institutional settlement rails;
- USDC distribution and transaction-share measurement;
- competition between issuer-linked L1s and neutral settlement networks;
- transmission from stablecoin infrastructure adoption to on-chain liquidity, chain activity and asset-specific breadth;
- concentration and governance risk in an institutionally permissioned validator set.

The event is not evidence of immediate crypto liquidity expansion. A bank charter, validator announcement or mainnet date is infrastructure capacity; realized transmission requires later evidence such as USDC circulation growth, net issuance, bridge flows, Arc production activity, institutional settlement volume and cross-chain displacement.

The next normal DATA PING cannot answer the mechanism or historical-base-rate questions, particularly because the current stablecoin global-total sensor remains unavailable. A dedicated structural research package is therefore justified before the next major framework review.
