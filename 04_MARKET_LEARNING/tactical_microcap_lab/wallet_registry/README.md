# Wallet Intelligence Registry

Persistent, address-first research records for wallets or clusters that become materially relevant across Meme Alpha cases.

Rules:

- Do not create a record merely because a wallet appears in one holder list.
- Candidate records should arise from decision-relevant evidence such as unusually early entries, disciplined exits, launch privilege, recurring co-trading or direct creator/deployer links.
- Include failed trades and dead launches where observable, not only winners.
- Freeze `REPEATABLE_EDGE_CANDIDATE` state before future outcomes are known.
- Keep wallet skill and connected-wallet relationship evidence as separate axes.
- Public wallet addresses may be stored here. Do not store private source identities.
- Use `../schemas/WALLET_ALPHA_RECORD_V1.json` as the minimum shape for machine-readable records.

No wallet registry entry grants automatic trade authority.
