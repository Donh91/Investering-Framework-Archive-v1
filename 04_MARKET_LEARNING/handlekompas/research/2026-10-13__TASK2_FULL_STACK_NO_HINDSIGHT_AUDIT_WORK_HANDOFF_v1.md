## Objective and scientific contract

Investigate what worked and failed across **all tracked market signals**, not just forecast direction:
1. Could the signal actually have been known and issued before the later outcome?
2. Did it add incremental forecast information beyond cheap, same-clock naive controls?
3. Did it change a legitimate action, at a feasible investor delivery and execution time?
4. Did the path subsequently reward that action net of costs, missed upside, false exits, capital-at-risk and re-entry friction?
5. Was that result stable across source versions, regimes and genuinely independent event clusters?
6. Did the chat, Cycle Navigator and site display the same eligible frozen evidence without accidental stronger claims?
7. Did learning feed back to the right governed owner, including negative results, with no self-approval or hindsight replacement?

**Mandatory negative answer:** UNKNOWN/NO_EVIDENCE whenever the required original issue clock, admitted first-known source, original target, outcome, action entitlement, counterfactual execution basis, privacy authority or independent family is absent. A 100% successful synthetic test is only a test of code behavior. More features, variants, time periods or overlapping coin horizons do not manufacture independent N.
