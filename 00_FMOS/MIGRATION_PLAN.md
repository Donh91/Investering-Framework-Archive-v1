# FMOS v0.2 — Preservation-First Migration

## Stage 0 — Freeze and inventory
Read-only discovery of repositories, owner files, ledgers, schedules, workflows and canonical pointers. Produce owner registry and legacy map. No moves or renames.

## Stage 1 — Schema and CI foundation
Create schemas, registries and validators for receipts, raw capture, evidence atoms, edge records, conflicts, retrieval bundles, replay manifests, promotions and tombstones.

## Stage 2 — DATA PING shadow capture
Only DATA PING main. Deterministic path. Readback verification mandatory. No retrieval consumption, promotion or owner writes.

Acceptance: >=99% verified capture; zero invalid merged objects; duplicate collapse 100% on fixtures; deterministic rebuild byte parity.

## Stage 3 — Research Lab sidecar
Add versioned LLM extraction capped at A1. Add root ancestry, Claude sample audit, quarantine and redaction drill.

## Stage 4 — Graph and retrieval shadow
Build graph, indexes and bundles. Compare against manual GitHub retrieval. Bundles remain audit-only.

## Stage 5 — Master Monday integration
Supply bounded weekly bundles and maturity workpacks. Master Monday remains owner. Measure reduced missed context, duplicate work and adjudication latency.

## Stage 6 — Owner adapters
Add one owner system at a time. Adapters produce proposals or materialized copies with routing receipts. No implicit authority transition.

## Stage 7 — Full machine brain
Enable cross-domain retrieval, point-in-time replay, calibration learning and graph-assisted research routing. Keep portfolio action and canonical decisions in owner governance.

## Rollback

Stop capture and consumers; freeze queues without deleting; revoke automation tokens; preserve immutable evidence; remove/rebuild derived views; use governed tombstones for privacy incidents; restore owner consumption to pre-FMOS behavior.

Existing framework operation, DATA PING and Master Monday continue throughout every stage.
