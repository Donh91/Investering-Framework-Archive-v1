# MASTER MONDAY PROCESS

Status: Active operational rule
Date added: 2026-07-05
Effective from: 2026-07-05
Source context: ChatGPT
Applies to: Master Monday, Weekly RAW, Forecast Ledger, Cycle Navigator, Archive Sync

## Executive summary

Master Monday is the weekly synthesis layer.

It should combine verified actuals, active DATA PING context, Forecast Ledger learning, Sequence/PTR progress, Research Lab notes and Cycle Navigator needs.

## Process

1. Confirm active DATA PING version.
2. Review verified weekly actuals.
3. Review open RAW rows.
4. Review Forecast Ledger outcomes.
5. Review Sequence/PTR state.
6. Review FNP if relevant.
7. Review source conflicts.
8. Review Research Lab or shadow updates.
9. Prepare Cycle Navigator source state.
10. Summarize final operational state.

## Operational implication

Master Monday should be synthesis, not raw data dumping.

## Governance notes

Do not finalize Master Monday if active DATA PING version or verified actuals are unclear.
