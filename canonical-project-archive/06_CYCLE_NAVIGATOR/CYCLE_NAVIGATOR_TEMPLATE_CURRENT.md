## Executive summary

Cycle Navigator should read like a weekly market weather forecast.

Every section must have a clear time horizon and a clear purpose.

## Locked structure

1. Weekly Precision Score.
2. Market State.
3. Altcoin Cycle.
4. Altseason Countdown or staged rotation timeline.
5. Market Cycle.
6. Market Outlook.
7. Intraday Price Map.
8. Weekly Action Plan.
9. 2-3 Week Compass.
10. Model Evaluation.
11. Key Takeaway.

## Communication rule

Do not use vague regime labels without time horizon.

Explain:

- what was expected
- what actually happened
- what was right
- what missed
- what unlocks the next phase

## Operational implication

The template should preserve continuity across weeks while allowing the adaptive learning section to change.
