# SHADOW AUTHORITY RULE

Status: Canonical
Date added: 2026-07-05
Effective from: 2026-07-05
Source context: ChatGPT
Applies to: Shadow rows, Research Lab, DATA PING processing, Forecast Ledger, Master Monday

## Executive summary

Shadow rows are observational memory.

They increase accountability and learning, but they do not modify official framework state by themselves.

## Canonical content

Shadow material may include:

- observations
- candidate rules
- stress tests
- failure modes
- calibration notes
- early warnings
- rejected ideas

Shadow material becomes operational only after explicit review through the relevant process:

- Forecast Ledger
- Weekly RAW Learning Snapshot
- Master Monday
- governance review
- user-approved canonical patch

## Operational implication

Shadow processing can be rich and automatic, but official state changes must remain deliberate.

## Governance notes

Do not let hidden rows silently change regime, rotation, rebuy, portfolio action or public Cycle Navigator state.
