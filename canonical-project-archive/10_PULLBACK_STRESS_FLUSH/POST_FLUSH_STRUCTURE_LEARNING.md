## Executive summary

Price stabilization after a flush is not the same as recovery.

Post-flush structure must be classified before the framework upgrades state.

## Structure states

Use these post-flush states:

- Vacuum
- Mixed
- Absorption

Vacuum:
Price bounces, but internals remain weak.

Mixed:
Some repair exists, but confirmation is incomplete.

Absorption:
Price stabilizes and internal indicators improve together.

## Confirmation factors

Review:

- reclaim quality
- OI behavior
- funding state
- breadth response
- ETH/BTC response
- BTC dominance behavior
- spot versus perp quality

## Operational implication

Recovery attempt can remain active while recovery confirmation stays locked.

## Governance notes

Do not treat a short squeeze as organic absorption unless internals confirm.
