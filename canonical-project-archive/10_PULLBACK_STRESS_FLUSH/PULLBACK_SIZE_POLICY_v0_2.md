# PULLBACK SIZE POLICY v0.2

Status: Active operational rule
Date added: 2026-07-05
Effective from: 2026-07-05
Source context: ChatGPT
Applies to: Pullback policy, mid-cap handling, Master Monday, DATA PING

## Executive summary

Moderate pullback does not automatically mean mid-cap REDUCE.

Pullback size must be interpreted with structure, liquidity, position quality and confirmation.

## Canonical content

Default interpretation:

- Mild pullback: observe.
- Moderate pullback: hold quality unless hard triggers activate.
- Large pullback: review risk and liquidity.
- Extreme pullback: defense first, then re-evaluate.

## Mid-cap rule

Mid caps default HOLD in moderate pullbacks unless:

1. at least two hard moderate-to-large triggers activate, or
2. the position is specifically weak, overweighted or illiquid.

## Operational implication

Avoid mechanical de-risking of quality mid-caps during normal volatility.

## Governance notes

This rule does not force buying. It blocks automatic sell logic from pullback size alone.
