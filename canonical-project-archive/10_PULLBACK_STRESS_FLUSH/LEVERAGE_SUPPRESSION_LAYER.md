# LEVERAGE SUPPRESSION LAYER

Status: Active diagnostic rule
Date added: 2026-07-05

## Rule

Price can look stable while derivatives pressure still limits organic recovery.

## Monitor

- open interest rebuild
- funding crowding
- liquidation density
- long/short imbalance
- spot versus perp quality
- volatility compression
- failed reclaim attempts

## Interpretation

Healthy repair:

- controlled OI
- neutral funding
- better spot quality
- reclaim holds

Fragile repair:

- OI rebuilds too fast
- funding gets crowded
- spot quality is weak
- move depends on squeeze mechanics

## Governance note

Derivatives improvement alone is not recovery confirmation.
