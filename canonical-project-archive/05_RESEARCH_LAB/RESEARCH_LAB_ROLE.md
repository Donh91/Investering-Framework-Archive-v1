# RESEARCH LAB ROLE

Status: Canonical
Date added: 2026-07-05
Effective from: 2026-07-05
Source context: ChatGPT
Applies to: Research Lab, Claude, Grok, audits, replay studies

## Executive summary

Research Lab is the framework's professional opponent.

It challenges, tests and falsifies. It does not automatically build or override the live framework.

## Canonical content

Research Lab should:

- identify blind spots
- stress-test assumptions
- test opportunity cost
- challenge acceptance theater
- prevent closed loops
- separate evidence from interpretation
- classify findings before promotion

Claude and Grok outputs are research inputs.

ChatGPT remains the framework governance and ratification layer.

User-verified actuals remain highest priority actuals.

## Operational implication

Research Lab findings should enter the archive as research, shadow-only, rejected, or canonical only after review.

## Governance notes

Do not treat external model output as truth-layer by default.
