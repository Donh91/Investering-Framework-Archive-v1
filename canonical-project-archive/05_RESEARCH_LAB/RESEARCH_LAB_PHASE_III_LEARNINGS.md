## Executive summary

Phase III found that the framework's main offensive weakness is not necessarily bad signals.

The weakness is that useful signals can be applied too uniformly across asset types and regimes.

## Canonical learning

1. BTC and altcoins have different offensive profiles.
2. BTC-specific signals may deserve separate evaluation.
3. Altcoin gates remain more dependent on ETH/BTC, breadth and deployment quality.
4. Uniform defensiveness can create false-negative cost.
5. Time-rigid confirmation can become expensive when regime shifts quickly.
6. Graduated deployment remains a promising shadow-observed improvement, not fully proven.

## Operational implication

Future research should distinguish between:

- BTC opportunity cost
- large-cap alt opportunity cost
- mid/small/micro opportunity cost
- time-based delay cost
- signal-quality cost
