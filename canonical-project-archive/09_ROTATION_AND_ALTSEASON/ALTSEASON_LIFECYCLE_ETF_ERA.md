## Executive summary

Altseason is not a single switch.

In ETF-era conditions, BTC absorption can remain healthy while ecosystem transmission remains weak.

## Lifecycle

Use this lifecycle:

1. WAIT
2. PREPARE
3. DEPLOY
4. PROTECT
5. WAIT / POST ROTATION

## Phase meaning

WAIT:
BTC can be healthy, but ecosystem transmission is unconfirmed.

PREPARE:
Early transmission improves, but survival is not yet proven.

DEPLOY:
Rotation survives with ETH/BTC persistence, breadth survival, BTC dominance deceleration and deployment quality.

PROTECT:
Broad participation matures and risk management becomes primary.

POST ROTATION:
Capital returns to defense, review and reset.

## Operational implication

Do not reduce the public or internal model to no rotation versus altseason.

Use staged capital behavior and staged communication.

## Governance notes

TechDev and macro context can support PREPARE WATCH, not DEPLOY by themselves.
