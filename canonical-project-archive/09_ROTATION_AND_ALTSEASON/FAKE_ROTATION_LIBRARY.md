## Executive summary

Fake rotation risk is highest when early signals appear without breadth survival and deployment quality.

## Core fake rotation types

Type 1 - BTC-only rally

- BTC rises
- ETH/BTC stays flat or weak
- BTC dominance rises

Interpretation: no rotation.

Type 2 - Narrative spike

- large caps pump
- breadth does not expand
- move is short-lived

Interpretation: selective or temporary strength only.

Type 3 - Combined fake rotation

- Pre-Trigger fires
- ETH/BTC spikes briefly
- BTC dominance stays high or reclaims
- breadth remains weak

Interpretation: highest-risk fake rotation.

## Diagnostic rule

If BTC dominance reclaims, breadth fails, deployment stays low or stablecoins remain parked during the survival window, do not upgrade rotation.

## Operational implication

Fake rotation rows should feed Weekly RAW, Master Monday and Cycle Navigator language.

## Governance notes

A fake rotation warning is not bearish by itself. It blocks premature rotation upgrades.
