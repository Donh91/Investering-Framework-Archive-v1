# Jev x Alpha Lab blind replay v1

Date: 2026-09-19
Status: FROZEN DESIGN / SHADOW RESEARCH ONLY
Owner: existing Alpha Lab research and outcome machinery
Jev role: challenger only

## North-star

Test whether pinned Jev can reduce expensive semantic routing work while preserving or improving critical evidence retention. Historical replay must not prove that Jev can predict pumps. It must test whether Jev chooses the cheapest correct next research action from the evidence that actually existed at cutoff.

## Unit and hindsight firewall

The replay unit is an immutable `MEME_ALPHA_G2_G3_SHADOW_OBSERVATION_v1` packet. The state builder accepts only frozen point-in-time fields, preserves UNKNOWN, rejects mutable historical substitutions, rejects outcome-like keys recursively and binds every state to the source observation SHA-256.

Outcomes are revealed only after every challenger prediction is frozen and hashed.

## Frozen primary judgments

Jev 1.13.0 receives six narrow judgments:

1. `material_evidence` - Noul: evidence merits additional research attention.
2. `evidence_conflict` - Noul: supplied evidence contains a material unresolved conflict.
3. `information_density` - Score 0-4: amount of decision-useful information in the supplied state.
4. `deep_dive_value` - Noul: existing specialist deep-dive machinery is likely worth invoking.
5. `preserve_verbatim` - Noul: evidence should be retained verbatim rather than compressed away.
6. `frontier_review_need` - Noul: ambiguity/conflict/value justifies scarce frontier-model review.

Identity, provenance truth, arithmetic, dates, sellability calculations and outcomes remain deterministic/existing-owner responsibilities.

## Challengers

Use the same blind state for existing Alpha Lab baseline, deterministic-only rules, fixed System One Adapter and pinned Jev 1.13.0. No challenger sees outcome data.

## Counterfactual routing

Replay routes are only `RETAIN`, `DEEP_DIVE` and `FRONTIER_REVIEW`. There is deliberately no DROP route in v1. Low certainty or low apparent value cannot silently delete evidence.

The initial composition thresholds are preregistered replay mechanics only. They carry zero production authority and must not be copied into live routing without held-out plus prospective evidence.

## Outcome reveal

Existing Alpha Lab outcome maturation owns truth: MFE, MAE, realizable return after slippage, exit feasibility, source/provenance resolution, falsifier result and missed-winner audit. Positive alpha requires sellability; ATH/touched MFE alone is insufficient.

## Primary gate

Safety dominates efficiency. First measure critical evidence loss, later-material false negatives, conflict misses, missed-winner recall and invariant violations. Then measure counterfactual frontier calls/tokens avoided, latency/cost and routing utility. Calibration/Brier is used only where the label is genuinely binary and point-in-time-clean.

## Kill

Immediate fail on outcome leakage, immutable-first-seen violation, silent critical evidence loss, identity/provenance delegation to Jev or restricted-data boundary violation. No promotion if incremental utility does not beat a simple baseline or disappears on held-out/missed-winner/adversarial slices.

A historical PASS can only open prospective live shadow. It cannot create production, portfolio or trading authority.
