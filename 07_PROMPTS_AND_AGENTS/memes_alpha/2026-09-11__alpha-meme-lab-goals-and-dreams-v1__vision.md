# Alpha Meme Lab — Goals & Dreams v1

**Date:** 2026-09-11  
**Status:** VISION / NON-AUTHORITATIVE TARGET STATE  
**Area:** Memes v3 / Alpha Lab  
**Authority:** NONE — goals, aspirations and future measurement targets only  
**Relationship:** Complements the existing Memes v3 research workstream, empirical-hardening addendum and Astra chain-native research compiler. It does not create a new engine, signal, execution path, portfolio authority or promotion rule.

## 1. Why this document exists

Freeze the current ambition for Alpha Meme Lab before later evidence, Astra audits and prospective outcomes change our view.

This file is intentionally aspirational. It records what we hope the project can become, so future versions can measure whether the system actually moved closer to these goals rather than rewriting the ambition after the fact.

The future evaluation question is not "did we build more machinery?" but:

> Did Alpha Meme Lab become materially better at identifying rare asymmetric opportunities early enough to matter, while becoming materially harder to fool?

## 2. Desired end-state

Alpha Meme Lab should not become another generic meme scanner.

The desired end-state is a specialist **asymmetry discovery + falsification layer** that sits on top of the broader Investering Framework.

Its job is to answer four increasingly difficult questions:

1. **Is this the right exact instance?**
2. **Is the capital and demand economically real and sufficiently independent?**
3. **Is the signal still under capacity and not already over-distributed?**
4. **Does demand survive the first meaningful distribution event?**

The system should prefer missing weak opportunities over confidently promoting a manipulated or already-crowded setup.

## 3. Near-future capabilities we want to achieve

### A. Extreme filtering

Move from large noisy candidate sets toward a very small number of cases that genuinely deserve attention.

A high-quality candidate should ideally come with:

- exact chain + CA/mint identity;
- correct narrative-instance binding;
- executable liquidity and route quality;
- conservative entity/cluster assessment;
- conditional wallet context rather than celebrity-wallet worship;
- narrative and propagation state;
- visibility/crowding state;
- signal-capacity estimate;
- explicit falsifier / kill case;
- provenance-backed unknowns.

The intended user experience is not more alerts. It is fewer, more consequential alerts.

### B. Early Confirmation as a state, not a timestamp

Develop a validated way to identify the earliest state where enough information exists to reduce manipulation and execution uncertainty while enough asymmetry remains to matter.

This should not be defined as a fixed minute count after launch.

The state may depend on:

- chain;
- venue;
- liquidity;
- entity structure;
- wallet behavior;
- narrative propagation;
- visibility/crowding;
- market regime.

The long-run goal is to distinguish:

`too early / unknowable -> early asymmetric confirmation -> momentum -> crowding -> chase`

with prospective evidence rather than hindsight.

### C. Conditional wallet intelligence

Replace the simplistic concept of a "smart wallet" with conditional evidence such as:

`wallet/entity × entry type × venue × token age × liquidity × narrative state × visibility × regime`

A wallet should be useful only where its own past, point-in-time behavior has shown repeatable incremental value.

Future target:

> know when a wallet matters, when it does not, and whether public visibility has already destroyed the useful part of the signal.

### D. Narrative Instance Selection

For narratives with many same-name or derivative tokens, learn whether the system can prospectively distinguish the exact implementation that becomes the coordination point.

Important research dimensions may include:

- first-mover timing;
- launch venue;
- liquidity quality;
- entity quality;
- wallet participation;
- distribution structure;
- social graph;
- ticker / semantic fit;
- community legitimacy;
- chain-native fit;
- competing sibling instances.

The system should never infer identity from ticker alone.

### E. First Distribution Survival

Learn to distinguish:

- first distribution as the beginning of terminal decay;
- healthy profit-taking that is absorbed by new independent demand;
- consolidation before another leg;
- false rebounds driven by the same linked capital.

This may become especially valuable after an asset has already moved substantially, where launch-sniper alpha is no longer available but a second asymmetric leg may still exist.

### F. Visibility and signal-capacity intelligence

Research whether an otherwise good signal becomes economically worse as it becomes more visible.

Preferred concept:

`signal_capacity_pressure = estimated_follower_notional / executable_depth`

Long-term goal:

> estimate whether an observable signal still belongs to us, or whether our ability to see it means we have already become someone else's exit liquidity.

Visibility must remain a testable adverse-selection feature, not an assumption that public signals are fake.

### G. Prospective learning moat

Build a private, irreversible research advantage through point-in-time evidence that cannot be honestly reconstructed later.

Every serious candidate should ideally produce:

- frozen pre-outcome packet;
- matched failures / rejected siblings;
- fixed outcome horizons;
- executable-return outcomes;
- MFE / MAE;
- liquidity and survival outcomes;
- whether the original thesis survived;
- whether a kill condition fired;
- what the system missed and why.

The aspiration is that six months of prospective evidence should make the system significantly more valuable even if no new data providers are added.

## 4. Desired relationship with the broader Investering Framework

Alpha Meme Lab should remain a specialist consumer of the broader framework, not a parallel macro engine.

Illustrative interaction:

### Cycle / regime layer
Determines whether the broader environment supports speculative high-beta risk.

### Rotation layer
Estimates whether capital is moving toward smaller-cap / meme-like risk.

### DATA PING / market-structure layer
Provides liquidity, breadth, volatility, derivatives and risk context.

### Alpha Meme Lab
Ranks and falsifies specific meme / microcap opportunities within that environment.

The ideal combined output is not simply "token X bullish".

The aspirational internal decision object looks more like:

```text
EXACT INSTANCE: verified
ENTRY STATE: early confirmation candidate
NARRATIVE: accelerating
INDEPENDENT CAPITAL: strong / uncertain / weak
ENTITY CONCENTRATION: acceptable / elevated / unknown
SIGNAL CROWDING: low / rising / saturated
EXECUTABLE LIQUIDITY: sufficient / marginal / insufficient
FIRST DISTRIBUTION: not reached / absorbing / failing
FRAMEWORK REGIME: supportive / neutral / hostile
PRIMARY FALSIFIER: explicit
UNKNOWN LOAD-BEARING FIELDS: explicit
```

This remains research context unless separately promoted through existing governance.

## 5. What Astra should ideally add

The dream is not that Astra merely produces longer reports.

Astra should be used to determine which parts of the entire research process actually add information.

Desired Astra behavior:

- audit every feature, agent, skill and research stage for incremental value;
- find duplicated or ceremonial complexity;
- identify which few features explain most robust discrimination;
- compare generic research against chain-native structured research;
- run specialist forensic / execution / narrative / bull / kill passes only when VOI justifies the cost;
- learn from rejected cases and failures, not only celebrated winners;
- simplify aggressively where complexity fails to earn its cost;
- propose bounded, falsifiable changes rather than wholesale redesigns.

A successful Astra audit may delete or demote many features. That would be a positive outcome if predictive and falsification quality improves.

## 6. Desired user experience

The ideal product for the owner is:

> Tell me only when something genuinely deserves my attention.

When a case qualifies, the system should be able to answer quickly:

- **Why this?**
- **Why now?**
- **How much edge may realistically remain?**
- **What would prove the case wrong?**

The goal is not maximal frequency, maximal complexity or maximal earliness.

The goal is high-value attention allocation.

## 7. Top-level dream outcome

The highest-level ambition is not simply to find more 100x tokens.

It is to become materially better at distinguishing:

> a genuine early asymmetric opportunity

from

> something that merely looks like one because of hindsight, manipulated wallet structure, fake distribution, thin execution, social echo, public crowding or narrative imitation.

The system should become progressively harder to fool while retaining enough speed to still capture meaningful asymmetry.

## 8. Future top-level measurement

A later dedicated measurement layer should score progress against this vision.

Do not define a vanity composite score yet.

Candidate future top-level measurements include:

- precision of high-priority alerts;
- recall of major asymmetric opportunities within the observable universe;
- false-positive rate and severe-loss avoidance;
- proportion of candidates rejected before terminal failure;
- incremental performance of Early Confirmation vs matched controls;
- executable rather than headline forward return;
- MDD / downside conditional on signal class;
- survival after first distribution;
- Narrative Instance Selection accuracy;
- incremental value of conditional wallet intelligence;
- degradation after public visibility / crowding;
- percentage of alerts where the stated falsifier later proved useful;
- prospective calibration of confidence;
- number of features / stages removed because they did not add value;
- research cost per genuinely useful finding;
- user attention burden per useful alert.

### Future "top measurement" principle

The eventual north-star metric should reward **useful asymmetric discrimination after execution and failure costs**, not raw hit rate, raw return, number of alerts or number of discovered winners.

The exact metric must be designed later from sufficient prospective evidence and should be frozen before being used to judge new model iterations.

## 9. Success criterion for the project vision

This vision is considered directionally achieved when Alpha Meme Lab can prospectively and reproducibly:

1. reduce a noisy universe to a small set of genuinely differentiated candidates;
2. explain why they are interesting using point-in-time evidence;
3. identify the correct exact instance more often than simple baselines;
4. distinguish real/independent demand from linked or manipulable activity better than raw address metrics;
5. measure whether a signal still has executable capacity;
6. reject a meaningful share of future failures before terminal outcomes;
7. identify which post-distribution survivors deserve renewed attention;
8. demonstrate incremental value when combined with the broader Investering Framework regime/rotation context;
9. improve over time from prospective outcomes without rewriting the past;
10. remain simple enough that every promoted component has earned its place empirically.

Until then, this document remains what it is intended to be:

**a frozen record of the goals and dreams we want future evidence to earn.**
