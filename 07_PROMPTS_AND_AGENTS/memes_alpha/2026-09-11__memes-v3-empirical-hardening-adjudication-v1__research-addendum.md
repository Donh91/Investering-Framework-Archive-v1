## 2. Updated Alpha Lab objective

Replace the weak mental model:

> find smart wallets and memes as early as possible

with the research objective:

> Detect a narrative early, bind the exact market instance, resolve economic entities behind addresses, measure whether capital and propagation are genuinely independent, estimate whether the signal still has executable capacity rather than being over-distributed, and observe whether demand survives first distribution before treating the pattern as repeatable alpha.

The workstream optimizes for **maximum information advantage per unit of unresolved risk**, not maximum earliness.

## 3. Canonical research principles

The following are methodology rules, not trade rules:

1. **Exact chain + CA/mint is identity.** Ticker/name is metadata only.
2. **Snapshot first, outcome later.** Discovery state is immutable; later evidence is appended, never back-written.
3. **`available_at` and `computed_at` are distinct.** Any feature not available at the decision cutoff is leakage.
4. **Cluster before count. Entity before address.** Raw wallet/holder counts are diagnostic only when coordination can materially change interpretation.
5. **Executable return before chart return.** Price/ATH/FDV outcomes require liquidity, fees, route, impact, latency and exit-capacity context.
6. **Matched failures are mandatory for general claims.** Winners alone cannot validate alpha.
7. **Rejection Ledger is required.** Rejected candidates remain observable counterfactuals rather than disappearing from history.
8. **Venue is a distribution.** A model or threshold validated on one venue/chain may not inherit authority elsewhere without domain validation.
9. **Deterministic load-bearing transforms are code-owned.** LLMs may interpret, but may not authoritatively convert epoch timestamps, decimals, token units, hashes or other deterministic fields.
10. **Missing cluster evidence is UNKNOWN, not CLEAN.** Absence of a detected relation is weak evidence of true independence.
11. **Wallet reputation is conditional and past-only.** Future wins may never improve a historical wallet score.
12. **Research accuracy and economic usefulness are separate.** A high AUC can coexist with negative realised edge after latency, crowding, fees and adverse selection.

## 5. Highest-priority shadow hypotheses

### H-A: De-manipulated Early Confirmation

Primary entry hypothesis:

> The best risk-adjusted entry state may occur after deterministic structure/entity/liquidity evidence becomes observable but before broad social consensus and signal capacity are exhausted.

This is a **state**, not a fixed number of minutes. Thresholds must be learned in exploration, frozen, then validated prospectively per venue.

### H-B: Conditional Wallet Alpha

Test wallet value as:

`wallet × chain × venue × entry_type × token_age × liquidity × narrative_state × regime × horizon`

and require incremental information beyond matched momentum/activity controls. No global elite-wallet label.

### H-C: Entity-adjusted structure

Test whether cluster-adjusted buyer/holder concentration and economic-buyer counts improve manipulation/failure discrimination beyond raw address metrics. Graph features are preferred for forensics but are not assumed to predict returns until tested.

### H-D: Organic Capital Acceleration

Test de-manipulated flow components separately before any aggregate score. Candidate components include depth-normalized unlinked net quote inflow, new economic-entity rate, entity diversity, liquidity growth, linked-flow share, creator-linked share and wash evidence.

### H-E: First Distribution Survival

Prospectively freeze the first observable distribution state and test liquidity retention, economic-buyer renewal, seller absorption, entity concentration change and later survival/reclaim. Reclaim itself is an outcome and may not leak into the earlier classification.

### H-F: Visibility / Adverse Selection

Public visibility is a candidate negative feature, not a canonical fact. Measure:

- first public visibility time;
- number/types of public tracker surfaces exposing the wallet/signal;
- estimated follower capital / executable depth;
- visibility growth;
- performance before vs after public labelling;
- trade-before-publication lag.

Do **not** hard-code a follower-count rule. The relevant object is capital pressure:

`estimated_follower_notional / executable_depth`

The CDAS 6-10 follower examples are sensitivity calculations under specific assumptions, not universal thresholds.

### H-G: Narrative Instance Selection

Maintain `narrative_id -> exact instances -> chain -> venue -> creation time -> trigger time` and test whether focality, genuine depth, entity diversity, provenance, semantic venue fit and non-price reasons to hold predict sibling outcomes before divergence.

### H-H: Cross-chain Narrative Selection

Treat each chain/venue as a separate market for the same narrative. Test same-trigger outcomes; do not maintain a universal best-chain ranking.

### H-I: Independent Propagation

Prefer source-lineage and independent-community spread over raw mention volume. Keep as lower-priority research until reliable continuous social capture exists.

## 6. CDAS adjudication

CDAS v1 is accepted as an **adversarial methodology layer**, not a new engine.

Accepted:

- coordination is relational, so per-wallet marginals cannot fully represent it;
- graph/entity reconstruction is prerequisite for claims about coordinated actors;
- signal capacity and adverse selection must be part of economic evaluation;
- distribution research must be split by actor/latency class;
- heterogeneous signal conjunctions should be tested rather than assuming one heuristic is decisive;
- public visibility deserves explicit measurement.

Narrowed / not canonical yet:

- `visibility is negative evidence` remains a hypothesis because the planted-signal prior and visibility likelihoods are not empirically calibrated;
- `GRA > DIS > ACC` is a testable prior for feature value, not an established return-alpha ordering;
- the CDAS follower breakeven counts are scenario outputs, not universal capacity limits;
- distribution asymmetry is only useful when entity clustering and latency make the event observable/actionable.

## 7. MemeTrans adjudication

MemeTrans remains valuable for coordination feature engineering and post-graduation risk research, but its sample is conditioned on Pump.fun migration/graduation. It must not be treated as representative of the ultra-early pre-graduation universe without transfer validation.

The reported AUPRC interpretation must be reproduced before using headline performance as evidence. Do not claim it is above or below the relevant baseline until the exact metric definition is resolved.

## 8. Prospective cohort preservation

The 21-instance cohort frozen at `2026-09-11T02:20:00Z` is a high-value prospective natural experiment.

Required outcome checkpoints, preserving the original rows even when aggregators stop returning a token:

- +24h: `2026-09-12T02:20:00Z`
- +7d: `2026-09-18T02:20:00Z`
- +30d: `2026-10-11T02:20:00Z`
- +60d: `2026-11-10T02:20:00Z`

`NOT_RETURNED` is a valid outcome and must never cause deletion of the frozen row.

Candidate exploratory ratios such as `liquidity / FDV` and `turnover / liquidity` must be measured at prospective cutoffs and tested against liquidity/size-matched controls. Their ability to describe a dead token after collapse is not evidence that they predicted the collapse.
