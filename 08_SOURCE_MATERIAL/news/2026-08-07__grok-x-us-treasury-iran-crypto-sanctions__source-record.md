## Candidate claim

Grok reports that Treasury/OFAC designated Shelbit Exchange and Aban Tether plus related operators for Iranian sanctions-evasion / illicit-finance activity supporting the IRGC.

## Main-thread primary-source crosscheck

U.S. Treasury press release dated 2026-08-07 confirms the action. Treasury states that it is acting against two major digital asset exchanges used by the Iranian regime and related network operators. It identifies Shelbit Exchange and Aban Tether. Treasury states that IRGC-linked addresses sent over $1M to Shelbit addresses, over $2M moved from Shelbit addresses to IRGC addresses, and Kayvanpour-controlled addresses sent over $2M to designated Nobitex. Treasury describes Aban Tether as an Iran-based digital asset exchange that processed millions of dollars involving previously designated Iranian exchanges.

Important identity clarification: `Aban Tether` is the name of an Iran-based exchange in the Treasury release; it is not evidence that Tether Ltd/USDT itself was designated.
