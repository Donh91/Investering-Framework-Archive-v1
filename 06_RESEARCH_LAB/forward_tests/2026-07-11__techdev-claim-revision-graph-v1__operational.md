## Graph conclusion

The archive shows both strengths and vulnerabilities:

- TechDev adapts and explicitly acknowledges some errors.
- The analytical system became more macro-aware and less dependent on the halving clock.
- Directional theses frequently survived through new analogs, degree changes or moved invalidations.
- Exact timing and rotation windows accumulated revisions more often than broad regime direction.
- Governance must reward useful adaptation without letting revisions erase the original forecast record.
