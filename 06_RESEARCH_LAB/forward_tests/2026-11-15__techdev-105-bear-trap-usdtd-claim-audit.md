## C. Critical interpretation of the four thesis pillars

### 1. BTC higher-timeframe retest / 2023 analogy
**Strength:** source presents a concrete support zone and an explicit approximately 77k 3-day confirmation target.  
**Weakness:** only favorable analog instances shown; choosing the matching start/end dates after observing the decline creates shape-anchoring. 'Rhyme' can be stretched to explain a deeper drop without limiting the model.  
**Test:** before outcome, freeze exact price normalization, temporal alignment, pattern recognition, divergence tolerance and invalidation; compare to simple BTC price/volatility controls across *all* historical wedge/retest cohorts, not 2023 alone.

### 2. ETH 3-day RSI near 24
**Strength:** extreme momentum stress can enrich a bounce/watchlist hypothesis.  
**Weakness:** oversold conditions can persist, and RSI depends on venue, candles, smoothing and exact reference close. A six-year extreme is not itself a conditional rebound win rate.  
**Test:** reconstruct 3D RSI on frozen venue-close data, define RSI <= 24 prereg cohort, report forward 3/7/14d excursions and MAE/MFE vs other equally large selloffs, with clustered-event effective N and holdouts. No independent check of the six-year ranking yet.

### 3. USDT.D + DXY lag + weekly RSI backtest
**Identity:** `USDT.D_t = MCAP_USDT_t / MCAP_ALL_CRYPTO_t`. Exactly in log units, `Δlog(USDT.D) = Δlog(MCAP_USDT) - Δlog(MCAP_ALL_CRYPTO)`. This is a mechanical accounting relationship, not proof of a leading capital-flow signal. A negative USDT.D return can occur while USDT dollars remain unchanged, merely because crypto prices expand the denominator. Conversely a rising ratio during a selloff does not by itself prove actual USDT buying/deployment.

**Issue-specific falsification design:** (i) freeze the chart universe and lag measured in calendar or trading weeks *before* seeing future outcomes; (ii) separately model USDT supply/market cap and total-cap changes; (iii) compare USDT.D level/weekly RSI break to naive BTC momentum, ETH/BTC, realized volatility, USDT issuance and total-cap momentum; (iv) check directional lead/lag on both sides of the DXY relation with a block-bootstrap and placebo lag sweep; (v) hold out new periods and charge multiple-testing; (vi) score whether USDT.D adds incremental prediction *beyond its denominator*. Explicitly report zero/negative incremental information if found.

### 4. ETF-era transmission, actionable recovery vs mechanical bounce
Sharp ETH oversold + BTC support tests may occur even while BTC.D is high, ETF flows negative and ETH/BTC weak. The framework's existing `BTC survival != ecosystem health` and ETH/BTC + breadth + deployment survival gates remain valid candidates, not truths immune to F12 falsification. Avoid self-fulfilling research: if a broad durable rebound occurs without existing gates, the false-negative/opportunity-cost ledger must record it as a miss, rather than reclassify the market to protect the default theory.
