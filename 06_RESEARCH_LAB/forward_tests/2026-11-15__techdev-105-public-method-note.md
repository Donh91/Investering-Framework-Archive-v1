## USDT dominance: denominator identity and falsifier design

Define `USDT.D = USDT market capitalization / total crypto market capitalization`, with a fixed provider universe and synchronized observation clock. By accounting identity:
`Δln(USDT.D) = Δln(USDT market capitalization) − Δln(total crypto market capitalization)`.

A falling ratio alone does not establish a causal capital-flow signal: it can be caused by appreciation of the denominator with unchanged USDT supply. Pre-register candidate horizons and lags, compare against USDT supply changes, total-cap momentum, BTC momentum, ETH/BTC and realized volatility, and use placebo lags, negative controls, rolling held-out periods and block-bootstrap uncertainty. **Falsifier:** no incremental forward information over same-clock denominator and simple price/volatility controls after multiplicity correction.
