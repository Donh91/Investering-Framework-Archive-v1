## Executive verdict

```yaml
macro_compass_value: REAL_BUT_CONDITIONAL
exact_timing_edge: NOT_DEMONSTRATED
price_target_edge: WEAK_IN_PRIORITY_SAMPLE
rotation_timing_edge: NOT_DEMONSTRATED
standalone_execution_authority: ZERO
topping_signal_role: LOCAL_HEAT_AND_RISK_CONTEXT
model_learning_quality: MEANINGFUL
revision_governance_risk: MATERIAL
```

TechDev is most useful as a broad macro narrative and roadmap challenger. The corpus does not justify treating exact dates, high price targets, altseason timing or mechanical trades as authoritative.

## What is genuinely strong

### 1. Long-horizon synthesis

The work integrates price structure, momentum, cross-market denominators, DXY, stablecoin dominance, liquidity and business-cycle data. This creates a richer scenario map than a single-indicator system.

### 2. Adaptation and explicit self-audit

The corpus contains real acknowledgements of wrong assumptions, overconfidence and failed structures. The migration away from a rigid four-year halving framework was intellectually useful.

### 3. Broad late-2022 regime read

The 15-20K bottom-region framing and expectation of a major 2023 recovery were among the most decision-relevant successes in the sample.

### 4. Conditional risk language

Many issues include invalidations, alternative paths and explicit statements that targets are not certainties. This is better governance than unconditional prediction.

### 5. Separation instincts

The BTC-first buying policy during downside and later recognition that alts need relative-strength confirmation align with the framework’s current separation of BTC permission from alt permission.

## What is fragile

### 1. Analog flexibility

When one analog failed, a new analog, timeframe, denominator or wave degree often preserved the same directional thesis. Adaptation can be valid, but it weakens claims of stable predictive edge.

### 2. Invalidation drift

Some hard-looking invalidations later became red flags, mid-term bearish states or reasons to change corrective labels. The original invalidation must remain scored.

### 3. Correlated confluence

RSI, MACD, moving averages, NUPL, liquidity and risk-asset measures often respond to the same underlying trend or liquidity regime. Counting each as independent evidence can overstate confidence.

### 4. Confirmation delay

Several strongest “confirmations” arrived after a meaningful portion of the move. A signal can be descriptively correct yet offer little incremental action value versus a simple trend baseline.

### 5. Price-target asymmetry

Large upside targets received detailed geometric justification, but expired misses were often followed by a new target family. The priority sample shows more weakness in target magnitude than in broad direction.

### 6. Rotation timing

BTC.D and ETH/BTC breakdown or reversal calls were often described as close or imminent across multiple periods. Durable broad alt rotation did not consistently follow in the stated windows.

### 7. Mechanical and discretionary mixing

The swing system evolved rapidly, and macro discretion sometimes overrode mechanical exits. That makes author-reported backtests and live results non-comparable unless every rule version is independently reconstructed.

### 8. Action ambiguity

A roadmap may be broadly right while providing a poor entry, exit or de-risk action. Framework-action impact must be scored separately from narrative accuracy.

## Counterfactual tests required before higher weight

```text
1. Does a TechDev macro state beat simple 200D or 20W trend state?
2. Does liquidity timing beat a no-timing broad window of equal length?
3. Do BTC.D and ETH/BTC calls beat first-cross and persistence baselines?
4. Does Top Gauge improve exit outcomes versus hold-core and simple drawdown rules?
5. Do target bands beat volatility-scaled naive ranges?
6. Do mechanical trades survive exact versioned-rule reproduction and transaction costs?
```

## Red-team conclusion

The strongest defensible claim is not that TechDev predicts exact tops. It is that the research can improve broad scenario awareness and force attention toward macro regime changes. The weakest and most dangerous use is converting highly confident analog, timing or target confluence directly into portfolio action.
