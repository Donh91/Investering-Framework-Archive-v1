## Executive finding

Historical **definitions** are materially more recoverable than historical **sensor states**.

The current repository contains strong modern sequence memory, point-in-time anchors, settled ETF calibration, breadth, stablecoin-liquidity and maturation ledgers. Those owner-grade retention layers are primarily July/August 2026 surfaces and therefore post-date the April/May legacy definitions.

No legacy event is scored merely because later market prices or modern proxy data can be obtained. That would answer what the sensor *might have said* under a modern reconstruction, not what the historical framework demonstrably knew at the time.

## Temporal evidence boundary

The modern Daily Capture Architecture explicitly distinguishes reconstructable hourly data from ephemeral point-in-time evidence and forbids interpolation and forward fill. Current permanent owners include hourly sequence, live anchors, settled ETF, breadth and stablecoin-liquidity surfaces, but the inspected retained directories are modern and do not establish April/May point-in-time legacy inputs.

## Scientific consequence

`UNTESTABLE` is the correct historical result where state identity cannot be recovered. This does not imply the hypothesis is false. It means historical evidence cannot support an edge claim.

The appropriate next move for promising concepts is a separately preregistered prospective test using the modern owner-grade capture architecture, negative controls and dependence tests.
