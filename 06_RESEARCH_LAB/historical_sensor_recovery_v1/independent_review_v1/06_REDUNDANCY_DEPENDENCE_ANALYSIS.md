# Redundancy & Dependence Analysis

## Main finding

The archive contains stronger evidence of dependence than of edge.

### Direct structural dependence

- Pre-Trigger is an explicit component of Rotation Readiness Score.
- Type 3 requires Pre-Trigger and reuses ETH/BTC, BTC.D and breadth.
- Breadth is an RRS component/modifier.
- BTC dominance is an RRS component and absolute-level modifier.
- The ETF-era divergence concepts share the latent factor `BTC absorption versus ecosystem transmission`.
- SRE and FAE share forecast/outcome lineage.
- ODM changes the maturation horizon on which SRE may appear successful or unsuccessful.

No empirical correlation coefficient is reported because there is no eligible historical sample.

## Special Test D — CCE

The distinction between `TRUE MULTI-SOURCE CONFIRMATION` and `MULTIPLE VIEWS OF SAME INFORMATION` is unresolved empirically, but the second risk is directly supported structurally.

ETH/BTC, breadth, TOTAL3/alt relative returns and dominance can all be manifestations of a common capital-rotation process. Likewise ETF flow, BTC survival and alt deterioration may be views of the same absorption/transmission regime.

Therefore CCE is retained as a **research control**. This is not evidence that a particular historical compression formula worked.

## RRS verdict

`ROTATION_READINESS_SCORE_V2` is currently `REDUNDANT` because it aggregates already-named components while the detailed historical subscore semantics and independent incremental value are missing. A future composite could only justify itself if it demonstrates calibration or information gain beyond its components.

## Design rule

Future scoring must report both:

1. raw confirmation count, and
2. dependence-adjusted confirmation groups.

A composite receives no extra credit merely for transforming the same underlying driver multiple times.
