# Regime-Conditional Analysis

## Historical regime performance

No regime-conditioned performance estimate is authorized because Stage 1 contains zero exact eligible historical event states. Therefore no percentage is reported for any regime.

The following regime slices remain legitimate **future test strata**, provided sample sizes are reported explicitly:

- BTC-led expansion
- ETH-led rotation
- broad alt expansion
- high BTC dominance
- falling BTC dominance
- ETF inflow regime
- ETF outflow regime
- risk-on macro
- risk-off macro
- high leverage
- low leverage
- high breadth
- weak breadth

## Sensor-family expectations that must remain hypotheses

- Pre-Trigger may be most relevant in high-BTC.D / pre-confirmation states, but this is not historically validated.
- Type 3 may function as a veto in high-BTC.D + weak-breadth environments, but the archived failure-rate claim is not reproduced.
- ETF-era divergence concepts are regime-specific by design, but their exact historical classifier identity is not recoverable.
- Macro Delay is inherently macro-regime conditional, but the historical transition clock is incomplete.
- RWE cannot be justified from these data because a regime-sensitive concept is not the same as a validated adaptive weighting algorithm.

## Rule for future reporting

Every regime claim must report `n` alongside any rate or effect estimate. If `n` is too small for a stable inference, the result remains `WATCH` or `REGIME_SPECIFIC`, not promoted.
