## Weekly interpretation rule

Registry review must distinguish:
1. `definition quality`,
2. `data completeness`,
3. `prospective sample maturity`,
4. `incremental information versus baseline`, and
5. `promotion authority`.

A strong definition is not evidence of edge. A mature sample is not promotion. Historical fit can never bypass the prospective firewall.
