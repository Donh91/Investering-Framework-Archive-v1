# Provenance & Leakage Audit

## Verdict

The frozen Stage 1 package passes the most important scientific test: it refuses to convert later, cleaner repository evidence into earlier historical sensor states.

## Leakage attacks performed conceptually against the frozen evidence

- **Timestamp leakage:** no historical event is scored without an observation timestamp and information cutoff. Result: no eligible events.
- **Famous-date leakage:** no famous cycle dates are used as event anchors because event identity itself is missing.
- **Later-document leakage:** current recovery syntheses are treated as indexes, not proof of earlier sensor state.
- **Current-definition leakage:** later source reconciliations are not silently projected backward, especially for BTC dominance.
- **Survivor/selection bias:** archived success claims are not accepted without a complete event ledger.
- **Hindsight thresholds:** no thresholds are tuned against outcomes.
- **Missing negative examples:** explicitly unresolved; absence of a complete event universe prevents rate estimation.
- **Version mixing:** April semantics and later source changes are separated where a conflict is known.
- **Proxy substitution:** modern breadth, ETF and market-owner data are not substituted for missing April/May states.
- **Future-aware labels/outcomes embedded in source:** no outcome join is authorized because sensor states are not frozen.

## Most important provenance defects

1. Pre-Trigger lacks point-in-time `stablecoin_alt_inflow_3d` and `large_cap_alt_volume_share_3d` observations.
2. Type 3 inherits the missing Pre-Trigger state and also lacks defensible historical breadth provenance and BTC.D source identity.
3. Historical breadth membership is not retained at the required large/mid/small state resolution.
4. ETF-era classifier semantics were not frozen contemporaneously for the proposed historical periods.
5. Meta-learning engines lack clean contemporaneous forecast-input-outcome lineage.
6. RWE lacks a reconstructable historical weighting algorithm.

## Consequence

Any historical performance claim generated despite these defects would be methodologically invalid. The correct outcome is `UNTESTABLE`, not synthetic reconstruction.
