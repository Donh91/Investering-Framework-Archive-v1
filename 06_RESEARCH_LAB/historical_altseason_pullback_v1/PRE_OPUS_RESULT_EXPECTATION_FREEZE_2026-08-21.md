## Prior expectations

### E1 — No single magic altseason trigger
Confidence: HIGH
Expectation: The strongest result will be conditional and multi-factor rather than one indicator or one fixed threshold. Breadth, ETH/BTC/relative strength, market regime and sentiment/liquidity context should matter jointly.
Falsifier: A single pre-event observable robustly dominates across episodes, controls and perturbations with large incremental value.

### E2 — Breadth/participation should be more useful than isolated headline price strength
Confidence: HIGH
Expectation: Cross-sectional participation and expansion should distinguish healthier rotation from isolated BTC/ETH moves and false starts. Breadth may be especially useful as confirmation and persistence evidence.
Falsifier: Breadth adds negligible or negative out-of-sample-style/incremental information once simpler price/relative-strength variables are known.

### E3 — ETH/BTC should matter, but fixed levels should be less robust than direction/persistence/context
Confidence: HIGH
Expectation: ETH/BTC improvement should be informative for rotation, but famous fixed thresholds should not emerge as universal causal gates. Persistence, slope, acceleration and interaction with breadth/regime should be more defensible.
Falsifier: One fixed ETH/BTC level is stable across episodes and dominates directional/contextual representations.

### E4 — Best buying conditions may occur before an obvious confirmed altseason
Confidence: MEDIUM-HIGH
Expectation: Attractive R/R likely appears during late pullback/recovery/early rotation, when evidence is improving but broad public confirmation is incomplete. Waiting for full confirmation probably sacrifices upside, while buying too early raises falling-knife risk.
Falsifier: Fully confirmed rotation consistently provides equal or better forward R/R than earlier improving states.

### E5 — Pullback depth alone should be weak; recovery quality should matter
Confidence: HIGH
Expectation: 'Down X%' by itself will not reliably identify a good reload. Subsequent stabilization, breadth behavior, relative strength, momentum transition and/or flow confirmation should separate constructive pullbacks from continuing deterioration.
Falsifier: Drawdown magnitude alone performs robustly and interactions add little.

### E6 — Sentiment/CFGI should be contextual, not a standalone timing oracle
Confidence: MEDIUM-HIGH
Expectation: BTC/ETH CFGI may improve interpretation of capitulation, reset or crowded conditions, but should not be sufficient alone. Its strongest value may be nonlinear and conditional on price/breadth/rotation state.
Known limitation: historical MARKET CFGI is provider-unavailable and must remain NOT_TESTABLE, so no expectation is made for MARKET CFGI.
Falsifier: BTC/ETH CFGI alone robustly dominates broader market-state variables.

### E7 — Intraday execution variables should add some timing value but much less regime value
Confidence: MEDIUM
Expectation: VWAP, RVOL, opening range, 1h/4h momentum, taker flow, OI and funding may improve entry/trim timing inside an already-authorized window, but should not replace regime/rotation evidence. Incremental edge is expected to be modest and state-dependent.
Falsifier: Intraday variables independently and robustly identify the broader altseason/regime state or show no incremental execution value at all.

### E8 — Flow/derivatives signals should be asymmetric
Confidence: MEDIUM
Expectation: OI/funding/taker-flow extremes may be more useful for identifying fragile/crowded moves, false breakouts or poor execution moments than for positively predicting durable altseason by themselves.
Falsifier: Derivatives variables are symmetric, stable positive predictors of durable rotation.

### E9 — Interactions should matter more than adding many weak sensors
Confidence: HIGH
Expectation: A small number of coherent interactions should explain most usable historical information. The full feature universe may discover patterns, but a minimal sufficient stack should retain much of the robust value after destruction testing.
Falsifier: Large high-dimensional combinations remain materially superior after multiplicity controls, perturbations and episode-level robustness checks.

### E10 — Some intuitive signals will fail
Confidence: VERY HIGH
Expectation: Cowork should reject several plausible-looking relationships after controls. Null/negative findings are expected and desirable. In particular, famous thresholds, visually compelling chart patterns and isolated intraday indicators are vulnerable to overfitting.
Falsifier: Nearly every intuitive family appears positive and robust. That outcome would itself raise methodological concern.

### E11 — Lead/lag structure should be heterogeneous across episodes
Confidence: HIGH
Expectation: There will not be one universal 'N hours/days before altseason' timing. Useful signals should cluster into stages, deterioration/reset, stabilization, early relative-strength improvement, breadth expansion, then broader confirmation.
Falsifier: A narrow universal lead time appears stable across independent episodes.

### E12 — False positives should concentrate in incomplete participation / failed persistence states
Confidence: MEDIUM-HIGH
Expectation: Failed rotations should often show one or more of: narrow participation, ETH/BTC improvement without persistence, BTC-dominant strength, temporary momentum without breadth follow-through, crowded derivatives, or rapid loss of recovery structure.
Falsifier: Failed and successful rotations are indistinguishable on these dimensions.

### E13 — Opportunity-cost analysis will argue against excessive trimming
Confidence: MEDIUM
Expectation: Some defensive signals will avoid drawdowns but create costly false trims/missed upside. The best execution logic should therefore improve downside/entry quality without repeatedly exiting healthy continuation.
Falsifier: Defensive trims reduce drawdown with negligible missed-upside cost across episodes.

### E14 — Historical evidence will support candidates, not production rules
Confidence: VERY HIGH
Expectation: Even strong findings should remain FORWARD_TEST candidates because episode count/effective sample size, multiple testing and regime dependence limit causal certainty.
Falsifier: None within this research contract; automatic historical promotion would violate the frozen authority boundary.

## Expected ranking of likely useful families

This is deliberately qualitative, not a fabricated score:

1. Breadth / cross-sectional participation / persistence
2. ETH/BTC and relative-strength transition, especially direction + persistence
3. Joint regime/context interactions rather than isolated thresholds
4. Pullback recovery structure and momentum transition
5. Sentiment/CFGI as conditional context
6. Intraday VWAP/RVOL/momentum for execution refinement
7. Derivatives/flow primarily as fragility or confirmation evidence
8. Fixed famous levels and isolated single-indicator rules, expected to be least robust
