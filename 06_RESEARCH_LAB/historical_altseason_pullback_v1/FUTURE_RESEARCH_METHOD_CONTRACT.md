# Future pullback research method contract

Status: RESEARCH_ONLY / PRE-REGISTRATION_GUARD
Date: 2026-08-22

This contract is created after Round 2 and before any new historical search on new information dimensions. It exists to prevent the lessons from Rounds 1–2 from being forgotten or silently relaxed.

## 1. Inferential unit

Independent market episodes, not hourly rows and not assets, are the inferential unit unless a new design justifies another unit explicitly.

## 2. Persistence / lead criteria

No future candidate may be promoted merely because a threshold holds for N consecutive adjacent hours.

If a persistence/run statistic is used, it must be calibrated against a dependence-preserving null, preferably event-label permutation with the same full family/search process rerun inside each permutation.

Required reporting includes:
- observed maximum statistic
- null median
- null p95 or stronger family-wise reference
- family-wise p-value
- effective overlap/autocorrelation explanation for rolling features
- separate actionable-window result

## 3. Search width

Search width must be declared before running. Broad feature mining cannot be defended post hoc by single-cell nominal p-values or isolated AUC spikes.

Prefer one of:
- a small pre-registered family of exact hypotheses, or
- a broad discovery family with full max-statistic/family-wise correction and no retroactive confirmatory language.

## 4. Actionable window

Every timing claim must report a pre-declared actionable decision window separately from a broad descriptive pre-event window. Post-top discrimination cannot be described as precursor edge.

## 5. Episode definition candidate freeze

The current frozen production-independent research definition remains V0 for existing Round-1/Round-2 results.

For a future independently governed research programme, pre-register the following candidate BEFORE inspecting signal performance:

`V2_RESEARCH_LABEL_CANDIDATE`:
- preserve the existing drawdown trigger semantics;
- close the episode when 0.75 recovery is achieved OR 336 hours have elapsed, whichever occurs first;
- rebuild episodes, controls and anchors from scratch;
- use a fresh multiplicity budget;
- do not rescore old candidates and select whichever label gives better results.

Reason for candidate status: Round 2 found V2 preserved ~95.7% of frozen episodes while materially improving modern-era episode availability. This is a testability repair, not evidence of market edge.

Maximum status today: `RESEARCH_LABEL_CANDIDATE_ONLY`.

## 6. New information dimensions

Next historical work should prioritise information that is not just another deterministic transform of price/volume/taker-share, including:
- open interest and OI changes
- funding and basis
- order-book depth / imbalance / liquidity withdrawal
- stablecoin exchange inflow/outflow
- cross-venue positioning / divergence

Each data family must carry source provenance, timestamp semantics, availability/missingness audit and no-lookahead rules before hypothesis testing.

## 7. HOLD benchmark

For trim/reload work, HOLD remains the mandatory benchmark. Report hit rate together with opportunity cost, duty cycle, execution delay sensitivity and friction. A high hit rate cannot substitute for positive economic value versus HOLD.

## 8. Promotion boundary

Historical work cannot directly change production rules, thresholds, weights, policy semantics or portfolio authority.

Maximum historical classification: `FORWARD_TEST`.

Promotion requires a separate review and, where appropriate, prospective evidence.
