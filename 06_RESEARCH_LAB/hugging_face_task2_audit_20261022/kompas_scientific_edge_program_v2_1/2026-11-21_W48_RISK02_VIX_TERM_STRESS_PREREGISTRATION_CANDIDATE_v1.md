## 4. Endpoint and falsification fixed in advance

For each eligible decision on the exact HH:00Z grid, P0 is the **shared as-of admissible reference CLOSE** from §3, never the just-finished or later-arriving hour. Primary candidate event = LOW <=0.95 x P0 in ANY of the **72 consecutive hourly spot bars opening at HH:00Z, HH+1:00Z, ... HH+71:00Z** and all fully matured when scored. Only bars completed after the decision are eligible. Record the FIRST qualifying LOW bar's closing UTC time as the crossing/onset candidate. If an hour is missing or the 72nd has not completed, mark UNMATURED/SOURCE_BLOCKED rather than a negative. Report event onset, first actionable alert lead, adverse excursion and maturity. Same asset, exchange/venue, USDT quote and exposure for all arms. Historical HTTP retrieval is NOT proof of old provider first-known or prior consumer availability.

**Deterministic episode registry, independent of warning arms:** create event candidates ONLY from fully mature, qualifying decision windows (LOW <=0.95 x the pinned P0), using each window's FIRST qualifying future bar completion time. Deduplicate identical crossing timestamps per asset. Sort candidates per asset ascending; the earliest unused crossing is episode onset t0, and all candidate crossings with timestamp <t0+72h belong to that episode; the first candidate >=t0+72h starts the next episode. NEVER recursively extend t0 to chain indefinitely. Across BTC/ETH, sort asset-episode onsets; a family anchored at earliest t0 includes other asset onsets <=t0+24h, without recursive extension; the next onset outside that fixed bound starts a new family. Families are the independent inference units; hours and correlated assets are not independent N. A fully mature decision is a quiet negative control only when none of its own 72h outcome bars qualifies.

**Warning matching:** For each arm and asset, a warning can match the earliest as-yet-unmatched eligible episode only if its decision precedes onset by at most 72h AND its own complete §4 outcome has `LOW <= 0.95 × warning_P0` within its own 72h horizon. The warning's first qualifying crossing must belong to that same asset episode under the fixed registry; a crossing whose anchor comes only from another decision or another asset supplies no credit. An earlier unmatched or immature episode cannot override a warning's own clock or endpoint. Prefer earliest eligible warning if multiple, count later duplicates (including across ISO-week budget resets) as redundant warning cost without double-counting false warnings, and never credit one alert to two events. An unmatched nonredundant warning with a fully mature eligible window is false; an unmatched event is missed. Missing or immature outcomes remain UNKNOWN. Freeze the registry, matching and denominators before future outcomes.

Synthetic boundary cases: an earlier eligible reference of 100 yields onset at a low of 95. A prior warning whose own pinned reference is 101 can match if that crossing is within its complete 72-hour window and the same asset episode. A prior warning with reference 95 and minimum low 95 over its own 72 hours is false even though it precedes that episode (its threshold is 90.25). A qualifying low first occurring after the warning's 72nd bar supplies no match.

Primary abstract cost: each false warning costs 1 unit; each missed eligible adverse event costs 3 units. Report paired sensitivity with missed-event costs 1 and 5 units, without selecting the favorable ratio post hoc. This is NOT trading PnL or an empirical portfolio cost. **Separate earlier-warning estimand:** for every independently matched family detected by BOTH C2 and comparator X (X=B0 and X=B1 separately), calculate paired lead difference Δlead_X = (t_onset - first_C2_warning) - (t_onset - first_X_warning) in hours. Extra C2-detected families that X missed count toward recall/cost ONLY, not as infinite or imputed lead advantage. Predeclare timing as NOT_ESTIMABLE if fewer than three jointly detected families for either comparison. Primary superiority requires C2 LOWER 1:3 cost than BOTH, no higher false-warning rate at shared exposure and equal maximum budget, AND strictly positive median paired Δlead against EACH comparator on at least three jointly detected families. Otherwise NOT_SUPERIOR/NOT_ESTIMABLE; don't call extra detections earlier warning. Report joint-N, paired leads, incremental catches, recall, precision, quiet-hour false alarms, suppressed alerts, absent/late source hours and cluster uncertainty. No pooled-hour significance.

Secondary opportunity cost: quiet/upward-market warning burden, reported separately from the adverse-low endpoint. No source-safe executable price or fill claim is implied by an abstract warning. Round 3 remains prospective collection only; no hypothesis testing, PnL, effect estimation or outcome scoring on Round 3 data.
