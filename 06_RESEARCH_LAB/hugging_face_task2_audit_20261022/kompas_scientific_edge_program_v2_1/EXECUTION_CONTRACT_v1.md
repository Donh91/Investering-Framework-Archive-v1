## Explicit source and scientific admit gates

`G_SOURCE`: exact source identity, quote and original version, original issue cutoff, no post-cutoff observation/retrieval, provider original first-publication/revision (or independently admissible original source anchor). Distinguish source health from historical eligible period. If not independently established: `PIT_UNVERIFIED`.

`G_COHORT`: full original issued/NOT_ISSUED/abstained/unavailable/no-alert denominator by horizon/model, originally censored/unknown retained, overlapping 72h/7d labels not falsely independent. Per-season/cycle independent episode grouping explicit. A 257-run census or 824 price joins alone do not imply 257 or 824 valid forecasts.

`G_SCORE`: original forecast vs persistence, previous packet, momentum, volatility/range (and no-action when relevant) same asset, quote, exact cutoff and eligible outcome. Proper interval accuracy AND width/sharpness, confidence when originally emitted, no fake uncertainty. Chronological walk-forward and label embargo, pre-frozen multiplicity correction. Report denominator, independent event N and uncertainty; no source qualifies = NO_ADMITTED_EDGE, not zero actual model ability.

`G_WARNING`: warning publication vs first actual user-visible delivery (or UNKNOWN); eligible intervention time, negative no-warning hours, false/late/missed warnings. Historical 3/5→4/5 alternatives MUST have original available multisource evidence and no hindsight chosen cutoff. Warning ≠ trade.

`G_ECONOMICS`: after-cost paper-only filled/no-filled, latency, fees/slippage/capacity and exit/re-entry constraints, matched HOLD and adverse/exposure/missed-upside. Real wallet holdings or realized investor profits not required and never inferred. Missing delivery or fill -> AFTER_COST_UNIDENTIFIABLE.

`G_RELEASE`: original owner authorized edit scope, actual current branch/head, CI exact PR-event, binding review/protection, independent source/recovery for high impact, expected-head merge, actual main SHA/file blob, original consumer and safe rollback plan. No runtime S1 attribution without scheduled saved-task provenance.

**Findings classification**:
- FIX_NOW, BUILD_SHADOW, SOURCE_REPAIR, AWAIT_MATURITY, SCIENTIFIC_BLOCK, REJECTED, SUPERSEDED, PRODUCTION_READY.
- Distinguish source verified, simulation reproducible, prediction edge, actionable investor utility, production release and natural consumer. A structural validator PASS certifies none of the latter.
