## 4. Five critical falsification propositions

**F1: "The current historical miss proves a new model is required." REJECTED as stated.** W46's 33.69 and zero day-5-to-7 containment show a material original product miss under its declared scoring method. They do not identify whether the cause was source first-known, baseline, variance, placement, action mapping or market tail; no unseen new model was compared. Cheapest next action is complete PIT and competitor alignment via existing P1, not engine creation.

**F2: "A completed-close floor breach is a predictive warning." REJECTED.** It is a retrospective factual status once the close completes. P1-0 improves truth and user delivery, not lead time. A forward warning requires a separately originally issued, as-of eligible event, controlled comparator and full no-warning denominator under #1553.

**F3: "All model agreement is additional independent confirmation." REJECTED as unconditional.** Correlated inputs or mechanically inherited decisions produce pseudo-ensemble confidence. Before showing ensemble disagreement, require lineage family census and an equal-clock B1/no-change ablation; drop if no effect, classify descriptive only if useful for uncertainty.

**F4: "Longer horizon path should ship with probabilities now." REJECTED.** #1516 already specifies 2-3w and shadow 4-6w. Source-valid forecasts and calibrated probabilities are not demonstrated here. Conditional path may be displayed only from existing authority, with NO_EDGE/UNKNOWN when unavailable. Do not invent BASE/BULL/BEAR weights, tail depths or turning windows.

**F5: "Better Kompas context necessarily improves Alpha trade returns." REJECTED.** P3 one-way transport is a plumbing hypothesis until MAL P2a denominator, sellability, decision time, exit fill and costed maturities exist. Same-candidate no-regime paired benchmark is essential. Preserve VOID, FAILED_FILL and UNKNOWN as distinct populations.
