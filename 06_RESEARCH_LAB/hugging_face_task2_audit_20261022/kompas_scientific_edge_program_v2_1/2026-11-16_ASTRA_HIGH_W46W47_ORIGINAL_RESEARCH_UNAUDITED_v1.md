### Optional NON_EMPIRICAL sensitivity

For a purely hypothetical incremental defensive action, let:

- \(p\): fraction of warnings followed by a preventable adverse move;
- \(D\): benefit in that case;
- \(F\): missed recovery/opportunity loss otherwise;
- \(C\): incremental round-trip cost.

Under these simplifying assumptions:

\[
U=pD-(1-p)F-C,\qquad p_{\mathrm{break-even}}=\frac{F+C}{D+F}.
\]

For **invented sensitivity inputs only**, \(D=5\%\), \(F=2\%\), \(C=0.5\%\) gives break-even \(p=35.7\%\). At \(C=1\%\), it becomes \(42.9\%\).

These are **not empirical estimates, calibrated warning probabilities, feasible-fill claims, or verified PnL**. Actual delivery prices, re-entry rules, sizing, and execution costs could invalidate the simplification entirely.
