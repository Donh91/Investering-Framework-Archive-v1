## Priority experiments, without hindsight
1. Compare 2 public OHLCV feeds against known fixed Task 2 original forecast issue times, taking only bars available by each cutoff, no borrowing close_time from the future.
2. Test whether missing/censored original outcome denominator reproduces registry outcomes, not just headline accuracy.
3. Compare Polymarket probability to cheap mechanical baselines on market **open** states after excluding unresolved markets and fee-unknown rows; no inference from resolution alone.
4. Alpha Lab: split features by decision_ms and evidence first-seen; report false-positive rug and missed healthy launch under locked old/preregistered prospective cohort; no launch-spot BUY.
5. News: same-story cluster, first publish vs first provider retrieve, false rumors, market-first/news-first, and after-cost decision divergence. Never assign news early credit based on a subsequently scraped archive.

## Dataset acceptance fields
`candidate_id, hf_repo_id, revision_sha, file_path, file_hash, license_verified, upstream_provider, observation_ts, exchange/chain, venue, symbol/address identity, collection_start_utc, first_known_at_utc, revision_available_at_utc, published_at_utc, timezone, sample_count, total_denominator, missingness, censoring, path_status, asof_joins, drift, duplicate_events, restricted_plane_classification, test_owner, allowed_use, adverse_control, source_verdict`.

**Explicit blind spots:** We have not downloaded and counted all rows, verified all licenses, confirmed actual file SHA digests or evaluated performance. HF Hub listing metadata alone is not evidence of forecast utility.
