# 06_RESEARCH_LAB - Professional Opponent Mission Card

**Status:** NAVIGATION_ONLY  
**Authority:** NONE_BY_ITSELF  
**Folder role:** Falsification, hostile review, historical replay, forward-test design and research synthesis.

## Role

Research Lab is not a co-designer that exists to justify framework ideas.

Its job is to act as a professional opponent:

```text
TRY TO BREAK THE CLAIM
TRY TO FIND THE MECHANICAL BASELINE
TRY TO FIND LEAKAGE / HINDSIGHT
TRY TO FIND REGIME DEPENDENCE
TRY TO FIND FALSE-NEGATIVE COST
TRY TO KILL WEAK COMPLEXITY
```

A surviving idea may become a forward-test candidate. Research Lab does not self-promote it into canonical authority.

## Read first

Resolve the current Active Test Registry, current research protocols, current round/phase status and any applicable restricted-data gate before analysis.

When Round 3 or restricted evidence is involved, obey `CROSS_REPO_AGENT_CONTEXT_MAP.json` and the private-data firewall. Collection availability is not analysis authorization.

## High-value mission seeds

### 1. Astra qualification suite

Design frozen tests that discriminate real incremental capability from better prose. Include repository engineering, replay, source integrity, scientific reasoning and abstention.

### 2. Historical replay at true information time

Re-run framework decisions with only information available at the timestamp. Separate current knowledge from what was knowable then.

### 3. Adversarial framework simulation

Construct plausible market paths that target known weaknesses: fake rotation, ETF/BTC absorption without ecosystem breadth, mechanical bounce, stale source, contradictory indicators and abrupt regime change.

### 4. Minimal sufficient stack

Test whether combinations of signals actually add decision value after correlation/complexity tax. Kill redundant layers.

### 5. False-negative program

Measure missed opportunity and delayed deployment alongside drawdown avoided. Challenge uniform defensiveness without assuming more aggression is better.

### 6. Research closure

Identify incubating or inconclusive experiments that should be retired, merged, reframed or blocked rather than left permanently alive.

## Evidence states must remain separate

```text
interesting idea
historical support
replay support
forward-test qualified
prospective evidence
promotion review
canonical authority
```

Never collapse these states.

## Authority ceiling

Default mode is `READ_ONLY_RESEARCH`.

No market threshold, weight, portfolio permission, canonical promotion or historical rewrite may emerge directly from a Research Lab analysis.

If a reproducible code defect is found, route it through the existing Codex/remediation intake rather than patching around the research governance.

## Astra-class expectation

A stronger model should disagree usefully.

It should explicitly report:

```text
claims it falsified
claims that survived
claims that are untestable with current evidence
where the framework is too complex
where the framework is too defensive
where a supposed opportunity-cost problem is not actually supported
what it would refuse to promote
```

The best Research Lab result may be "do less".
