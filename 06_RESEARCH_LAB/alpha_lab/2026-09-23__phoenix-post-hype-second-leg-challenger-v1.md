## Source-derived hypothesis

A user-supplied X article proposes a post-hype continuation/re-entry pattern: an initial narrative/social expansion, profit-taking and cooldown, then an accumulation/support phase before a possible second expansion. Its example filters and chart patterns are inspiration only. They are not admitted as validated thresholds or edge.

## Framework interpretation

Do not build a new scanner. Test the idea inside the existing exact-CA lifecycle owner:

BIRTH -> EARLY_EXPANSION -> PEAK_FORMATION -> DRAWDOWN -> DORMANCY -> PHOENIX_WAKEUP -> PHOENIX_PRE_RECLAIM_CONVERGENCE -> PHOENIX_RECLAIM -> PHOENIX_RETEST_PENDING -> PHOENIX_RETEST_PASSED -> SECOND_LEG or FAILED_RECLAIM.

The useful question is not whether a chart forms a support zone. It is whether a token with verified prior demand survives distribution in economically executable form and then develops prospective evidence of renewed demand before broad re-propagation.

## Challenger thesis

Among exact-CA tokens with REAL_PRIOR_DEMAND, does a prospective combination of liquidity survival, sellability survival, bottom compression, declining sell pressure, improving buyer breadth and independent wallet re-entry before broad social re-propagation predict a realizable SECOND_LEG better than the existing Phoenix champion alone?

No component is sufficient by itself.

## Evidence families

T0/history-known:
- exact CA and token birth clock
- verified prior demand, not wick-only peak
- prior executable liquidity and successful sells
- prior buyer breadth and concentration
- prior peak and drawdown when reproducible
- project/CA identity and provenance state

Prospectively frozen recovery evidence:
- liquidity survival/change
- sellability
- bottom compression duration
- sell-pressure trajectory
- buyer-breadth acceleration
- holder/concentration change where healthy
- independent wallet re-entry, excluding gift/dust/privileged allocations
- reclaim attempt and retest state
- time relative to broad social/KOL re-propagation
- Cycle Navigator regime as stratification only

## Negative denominator

Retain:
- apparent accumulation followed by terminal decay
- dead/unsellable tokens
- liquidity collapse
- single-wallet mechanical volume
- failed reclaim
- reclaim without retest survival
- social revival without market survival
- wallet re-entry caused by gifts/transfers/privileged supply
- prior chart wick without REAL_PRIOR_DEMAND
- tokens that survive but never produce a second leg

## Edge gate

Do not claim edge from historical examples.

A challenger can advance only after prospective frozen observations show useful incremental discrimination versus the existing Phoenix champion, with failures retained. Required properties:
- no identity/provenance leakage
- no outcome-informed thresholds
- no ATH-only outcome
- no missing-data-as-zero
- realizable/sellable outcomes
- false-positive and missed-winner accounting
- evidence across more than one market regime before promotion

Until then state is UNPROVEN.

## Kill / revise

Kill or narrow if:
- apparent edge disappears after sellability/slippage
- failed-reclaim denominator removes the effect
- social propagation precedes the supposed early signal
- wallet re-entry is not independent
- liquidity survival alone explains all useful separation
- benefit exists only in one regime
- B does not add useful information beyond A
- source-inspired age/MC/liquidity/volume filters are required to manufacture the result

## Important design extension

Treat early launch discovery and second-leg recovery as two separate prospective opportunities on one immutable exact-CA history. A missed or intentionally avoided launch must not be rewritten as an early win if Phoenix later succeeds. Conversely, a successful launch call does not automatically grant Phoenix credit.

This permits two independent questions:
1. Can Moonshot/Project->CA find the project/token early?
2. If launch entry is missed or too risky, can Phoenix identify a safer later re-entry with independent prospective edge?

Shared evidence infrastructure is allowed. Training populations and performance credit remain separate.
