## Method and anti-hindsight rule

This packet does not call any row alpha. Current outcome or current market performance cannot create an eligible discovery signal. The question is narrower: did a public, timestampable project surface exist early enough that a project-first system could have retained it before or at token binding, and what matched controls show that the same surface is not sufficient?

Public project directories are discovery evidence, not truth. First-party/on-chain evidence is required for identity and exact CA. Current market snapshots are outcome context only.

The Robinhood Chain public testnet was announced 2026-02-10 with explicit focus on tokenized assets, lending and perpetuals and a $1m Open House/buildathon program. This makes buildathon/ecosystem project surfaces a legitimate historical discovery family, but not an investment signal.

## Feature survival after positive + negative matching

### Survives as discovery/project-memory evidence
1. Timestamped presence in an ecosystem/buildathon/directory can justify retaining a project candidate.
2. Shipped/live product surface is stronger than a narrative-only website.
3. Robinhood-specific primitive matters when concrete: stock-token reward flow, USDG settlement, chain-specific verified contracts, bridge identity, on-chain randomness, launch/factory relation.
4. Exact first-party/on-chain CA binding matters.
5. Material transition from no token -> exact public CA is useful.
6. Observable economic mechanism is more informative than generic category labels.

### Does NOT survive as alpha evidence by itself
- AI/privacy/RWA/agent keywords;
- polished website or documentation;
- open source or many tests;
- exact CA;
- fair-launch/no-premine claims;
- public ecosystem listing;
- 'live' label;
- community/social narrative;
- product quality without a project token;
- current price appreciation discovered after the fact.

## Falsification impact on Project→CA design

The project-first thesis survives, but in a narrower form:
- Project Memory should optimize recall of credible projects, not predict token winners.
- Project→CA binding solves identity/timing, not economic attractiveness.
- Alpha can only emerge after exact binding plus existing sellability/liquidity/traction/provenance gates and prospective outcome.
- The negative denominator must include no-token and non-investable projects. Excluding them would artificially inflate project-first precision.
- Product-live and chain-native should increase Deep-Dive value, not grant BUY/QUALIFIED_WATCH by themselves.
