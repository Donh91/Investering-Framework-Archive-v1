## Mission

Adversarially complete the design of the project-first discovery -> semantic triage -> exact-CA binding -> existing Alpha Lab verification bridge. The goal is earlier discovery of high-information projects such as the structural classes exposed by NET, SAFIX and DARK, without fitting rules to those winners or flooding Deep Dive with generic launches.

Assume the current proposal is wrong until it survives falsification.

## Frozen baseline

Champion: existing Alpha Lab / Moonshot Sentinel discovery and gates.
Challenger A: deterministic project-first discovery + persistent project watch + exact project→CA join.
Challenger B: A + narrow TypeSafe Jev semantic triage.
Challenger C: external adversarial architecture, frozen before prospective scoring.

NET, SAFIX and DARK are design/falsification cases only. They receive zero prospective credit.

## Architecture to test

1. DISCOVER - broad, cheap, deterministic/API collection of public project/product/launch surfaces.
2. NORMALIZE - point-in-time facts, exact timestamps, source health, UNKNOWN preservation, canonical state ordering.
3. SEMANTIC TRIAGE - Jev shadow-only atomic judgments.
4. PROJECT MEMORY - persistent candidate identity before a token exists.
5. PROJECT→CA JOIN - exact first-party/on-chain binding to chain, CA, factory, deployer and canonical pool. Ambiguity quarantines.
6. EXISTING ALPHA LAB - sellability, liquidity, age-normalized traction, provenance/entity, wallets, source/caller and catalyst verification.
7. DEEP DIVE / QUALIFIED WATCH - no BUY authority.
8. OUTCOME - prospective sellable MFE/MAE/realizable outcome and false-negative accounting.

## Jev role

Jev model pinned for experiment: jev-1.13.0.
Jev is not truth authority. It must not decide identity, provenance, arithmetic, dates, market cap, liquidity, sellability, CA linkage, BUY/SELL or threshold promotion.

Atomic questions:
- real_product_surface - concrete product/protocol with falsifiable mechanics vs generic marketing?
- chain_native_specificity - how specifically coupled to target-chain primitives?
- mechanic_falsifiability - how externally checkable are mechanics?
- incremental_information - new material information beyond ticker/name/social excitement?
- deep_dive_value - would existing specialist research likely add useful information?

Require canonical deterministic state order. Low confidence retains/escalates, never DROP. Preserve full distributions/confidence/latency/model version/state fingerprint.

## Deep Research questions

A. Discovery universe
- Which public/API surfaces expose emerging Robinhood-chain projects before or near token launch?
- Which are reproducible, timestampable, affordable and legally/operationally usable?
- Which surfaces existed early for NET/SAFIX/DARK, and which similar projects failed?

B. Project→CA identity
- What exact evidence can bind a pre-token project to a later CA without ticker/name leakage?
- Rank first-party publication, factory events, deployer relationships, website/docs updates, verified socials and contract references by reliability.
- Define conflict and quarantine semantics.

C. Anti-overfit denominator
- Build matched negative classes: serious docs but no token; product shipped but token failed; RWA/USDG narrative with poor liquidity; KOL-hyped failures; same-name/migration conflicts; rugs/unsellable; late successful projects.
- Determine whether proposed evidence families separate winners before outcomes.

D. Jev incremental utility
- Compare deterministic A vs A+Jev B on blinded states.
- Test representation order, prompt injection, marketing prose, conflicting sources, missing fields and borderline cases.
- Determine whether Jev improves Deep-Dive precision/load or recall enough to justify itself.
- Do not calibrate thresholds from NET/SAFIX/DARK.

E. Scanner intelligence
- Identify high-recall primitives that can be extracted deterministically.
- Identify semantic judgments where Jev adds information.
- Identify cases that require Sol/Deep Research rather than Jev.
- Minimize expensive calls and avoid a parallel scanner.

F. Prospective experiment
- Define immutable observation schema and candidate lifecycle.
- Define A/B/C routing with the same future universe.
- Define primary, safety and outcome endpoints.
- Define kill criteria before outcomes.
- Preserve all rejected candidates and zero-result days.

## Required metrics

Primary:
- incremental early recall vs champion
- false-negative rate
- precision among Deep-Dive candidates
- Deep-Dive load per eligible project
- lead time to correctly bound exact CA

Safety:
- rug/unsellable burden
- critical-evidence retention
- identity-conflict/false-link rate
- source-health failures
- UNKNOWN preservation
- cost amplification

Outcome:
- entry-age and entry-MC distribution
- sellable MFE / MAE
- realizable outcome
- time to public/KOL discovery
- outcome conditional on evidence family, not winner-only anecdotes

Minimum review target remains 30 future eligible projects, >=2 regimes and >=10 qualified watches.

## Kill criteria

Kill/redesign a component if:
- deterministic baseline matches it within preregistered margin;
- critical evidence is lost/downgraded;
- Jev route depends materially on representation order;
- injection/spoofing creates unsafe downgrade or unacceptable cost escalation;
- project→CA false-link burden is material;
- gains disappear after failed/similar-project denominator is included;
- advantage exists only for NET/SAFIX/DARK-like narratives;
- prospective held-out advantage fails;
- Deep-Dive load rises without useful recall/precision gain.
