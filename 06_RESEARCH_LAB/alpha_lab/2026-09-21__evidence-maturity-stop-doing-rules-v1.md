# Alpha Lab Evidence Maturity and Stop-Doing Rules v1

Date: 2026-09-21
Status: GOVERNANCE_RESEARCH / NO_RUNTIME_CHANGE
Owner: #1087

## Problem

Alpha Lab now has substantial architecture, hardening and historical research. The remaining risk is research accumulation itself: adding more clever layers before prospective evidence exists.

## Evidence maturity ladder

E0 - HYPOTHESIS
- idea, narrative, external claim.
- may create a research question only.

E1 - HISTORICAL_REPRESENTATION
- historical cases can be represented without leakage.
- zero alpha credit.

E2 - PREREGISTERED_CHALLENGER
- denominator, clocks, kill criteria and authority frozen before future outcomes.
- still zero edge credit.

E3 - LIVE_PROSPECTIVE_OBSERVATION
- future eligible rows accumulating with source health and immutable first seen.
- may report sample counts and data quality, not edge.

E4 - PROSPECTIVE_SIGNAL
- minimum sample/coverage gates met and challenger shows defined incremental effect.
- still shadow until independent review.

E5 - REPLICATED_EDGE
- survives second regime/time slice and negative controls, with sellability/realizable outcome.
- may be proposed for bounded promotion through governance.

E6 - PRODUCTION_QUALIFIED
- independent review, rollback, drift monitoring and explicit authority grant.
- not implied by research success.

## Stop-doing rules effective now

Until P1 or FOMO remediation produces executable prospective rows:
1. no new Alpha Lab architecture layer unless it closes a newly observed concrete failure;
2. no new historical winner case unless paired with a denominator/control or identity regression;
3. no new wallet cohort expansion from leaderboard performance;
4. no new model/JEV role;
5. no new score/weight/threshold;
6. no parallel scanner/ledger/forecast engine;
7. no 'edge' wording for E0-E3;
8. prioritize implementation review, source-health receipts and prospective data over theory.

## Research debt register

Open debt that is actually decision-relevant:
- P1 Project Memory code implementation and independent review.
- P2 implementation only after P1 acceptance.
- FOMO fail-closed remediation and independent review.
- explicit zero-counter activation receipts.
- source coverage instrumentation when P1 exists.
- future 30-trial Project→CA A/B/C population.
- future FOMO eligible convergence population and negative controls.

Everything else is secondary until new evidence creates a reason.

## Kill discipline

A challenger that reaches its frozen minimum population and fails its preregistered survival gate is KILLED or materially respecified. It must not remain indefinitely in 'learning' because the theory is attractive.

A source that is operationally unhealthy can be disabled for reliability/cost, but missing data cannot count against its discovery value.

A historical case can falsify representation or identity logic but cannot rescue a failed prospective challenger.
