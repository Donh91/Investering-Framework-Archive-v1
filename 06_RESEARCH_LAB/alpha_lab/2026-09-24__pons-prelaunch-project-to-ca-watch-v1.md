## Hypothesis

A credible project can become observable before its Pons token/CA exists. This creates a potential lead-time layer upstream of launch scanners:

credible project footprint -> explicit Pons launch intent -> pre-launch activity -> CA appears -> exact Project→CA binding -> first blocks -> independent demand

The purpose is to test whether this sequence adds measurable prospective information advantage versus waiting for the token Launch event.

## Mandatory freshness gate

The PONSTR false-fresh classification establishes a hard rule:

"launching soon" or newly surfaced project activity is not evidence of a new token.

Before a candidate can receive fresh/pre-launch status:
1. Search exact project/token identity for existing live CAs.
2. Search Pons/market/chain history for an earlier launch.
3. Keep project/social update age separate from token age.
4. Exact CA + canonical launch/deployment timestamp is required to establish token T0.
5. Until that is known, use PRELAUNCH_CA_UNKNOWN or AGE_UNKNOWN.
6. A new integration, listing, documentation update, social post or relaunch announcement never resets an old token's T0.
7. Relaunches remain a distinct cohort.

## Evidence to freeze before launch

For each candidate preserve:
- first observed explicit Pons-launch statement and timestamp;
- project URLs/repository/social identity;
- earliest project existence evidence;
- product/docs/repository state;
- waitlist/community/activity evidence where reproducible;
- tokenomics/utility claims as claims, not truth;
- whether an old token/CA exists;
- whether launch date/window is announced;
- authenticated first-party CA publication timestamp when it occurs;
- source health and evidence refs.

## Post-CA sequence

Once an authenticated CA appears:
1. exact Project→CA binding;
2. launch tx/block/time;
3. launch-origin/access-fairness;
4. supply/MC/FDV semantics;
5. liquidity + sellability;
6. buyer breadth and provenance;
7. seeded/mechanical vs independent demand;
8. wallet/caller convergence;
9. broad social propagation timestamp;
10. prospective outcome.

## Falsification

All candidates remain in denominator, including:
- never launches;
- delayed launches;
- identity conflicts;
- fake/copied CAs;
- launches with no independent demand;
- rugs/unsellable markets;
- launches that remain tiny;
- projects whose pre-hype was already broadly propagated and therefore offered no marginal lead.

No failed candidate may be silently removed.

## Success question

Does project-first pre-launch observation provide material lead time and/or qualification information that the existing launch-first champion did not already possess, before broad propagation and before market outcome?

A useful result can be either:
- earlier verified discovery of a later qualified opportunity, or
- useful early rejection of a seemingly credible pre-launch project.

No BUY/SELL authority. No production thresholds. No retrospective alpha credit.
