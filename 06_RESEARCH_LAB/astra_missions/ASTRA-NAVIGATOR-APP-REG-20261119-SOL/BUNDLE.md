## Owner goal (2026-11-19, verbatim intent, translated)

A well-functioning all-in-one app. It combines the framework's market compass (Kompas / Cycle Navigator), Alpha Lab Pro candidates and research, and a portfolio view. It also includes a wallet the owner can trade with: MetaMask-style, with gas, ETH, USDT and others.

The app and wallet are "a huge phase in itself". Research it now with the best server options, plans and execution ideas, so that a later long build run (Claude Fable) does not start from zero, while still being free to design and build what is needed.

## Binding constraints (existing framework rules; do not propose to bypass them)

1. **No autonomous fund movement.**
   - No AI, agent, server or scheduler may hold keys, sign, submit trades, withdraw or move funds.
   - The wallet is **non-custodial**: keys stay in the user's own wallet (MetaMask, a hardware wallet, or similar), and **the user signs every transaction**.
   - The app may *prepare* a transaction (asset, amount, route, slippage, gas estimate, expiry) for the user to review and sign.
2. **Automated trading of any kind is out of scope.** It would be a separate future owner decision with its own legal, security and risk review. You may describe what that decision would require, but do not design it as part of this phase.
3. **Data boundary.**
   - The private portfolio and wallet data of the owner never go into public repos, assets or logs.
   - The public CN site and the Alpha Lab Pro portal stay separate products. The app consumes their approved outputs through read adapters, not by scraping.
4. **No new market producers or scanners.** The app is a surface over the existing canonical owners.
5. **One developer plus AI agents.** Prefer boring, maintainable technology, and put operating cost and maintenance burden first.

## How to answer

- You have NO web access, so mark every time-sensitive fact `TO_VERIFY(<what>, <where to check>)`. That includes library versions, fees, gas prices, regulatory thresholds, API limits and prices.
- Distinguish VERIFIED_IN_BUNDLE, GENERAL_KNOWLEDGE and TO_VERIFY.
- Give decisions as ranked options with trade-offs, cost and a recommendation, plus concrete next experiments that are cheap, falsifiable and short.
- End with a machine-readable JSON array of decisions: `{id, decision, options, recommendation, confidence, to_verify[], depends_on[]}`.

Mobile-first private PWA, desktop research workspace; original visual language: midnight navy, neutral surfaces, blue forecast, green observed, restrained risk colors, high contrast, no hype, 390/430px responsive. Separate public CN site and Alpha Lab Pro portal remain owned by their respective producers; app consumes their approved backend outputs, not screenshots/HTML scraping.

TODAY: market phase, risk/pullback, weekly freeze vs current Kompas, one prioritized action queue and fresh/stale source labels. Swipe/expand for 12h/1-3d/5-7d/2-3w where canonical output exists. No invented horizons.

## Interaction rules
A card contains asset identity, horizon, provenance, evidence timestamp/expiry, scenario invalidation, net simulated economics, risk veto and reason, confidence status (not fabricated probability), link to original case and paper evidence. User can inspect readback and cancel an unsubmitted intent. Stale or conflicting source = visibly disabled action. Notifications material/deduped, no casino UX, no false precision. Settings: mode, paper capital, per-strategy budgets, exposure, data/AI spend, quiet notifications.
## Example (illustrative, not live)
Market: defensive; alpha opportunity: hypothetical token X; research complete but quote unverified; Risk: WATCH, blocked on sellability. Paper proposal disabled until valid quote. Details: original observation vs subsequent outcome shown on distinct clocks.
