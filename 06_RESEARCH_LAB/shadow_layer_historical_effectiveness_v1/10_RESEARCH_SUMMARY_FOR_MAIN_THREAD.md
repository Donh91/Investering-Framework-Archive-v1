# Research Summary for Main Thread

## What was actually researched

The review expanded beyond the 14 recovered legacy sensors and treated the full Shadow Layer as the research population, anchored in `04_MARKET_LEARNING/shadow_registry/` and extended through archived DATA PING, Claude Research Lab, Fable/OTA, breadth, entry signals, historical pullback research, ETF-era wiring and older Shadow Layer material.

## Main conclusion

The archive does not support the idea that every named shadow sensor has independent historical edge.

It supports a simpler and more useful conclusion:

**A small number of information families recur across many generations of the framework, while a larger number of named sensors appear to be transformations, composites, summaries or regime-specific uses of those same families.**

The strongest recurring information families are:

1. ETH/BTC persistence
2. breadth survival
3. BTC dominance path / reclaim behavior
4. ETF absorption quality versus ecosystem transmission
5. stablecoin deployment versus parking
6. leverage / liquidation / reclaim structure as timing context
7. sequence survival rather than first-signal detection
8. false-negative / opportunity-cost accounting

## Most important historical falsifications

- Fake Rotation Type 3's old ~55-75% failure-rate claim is not reproduced.
- Early Rotation Pre-Trigger's very high historical reliability language is not supported by clean event-level provenance.
- old microcap 75-85% failure claims should not be treated as established historical rates without event tables.
- old Shadow v1-v8 hit-rate/lead-time tables were explicitly synthesis, not statistical backtests.
- fixed historical BTC.D / ETHBTC thresholds must not be treated as timeless empirical laws.

## Most important surviving research hypotheses

### Rotation

A simple triad may contain most of the usable discrimination:

`ETHBTC persistence + breadth survival + BTC.D path`

Historical F12.5 replay material strengthens this hypothesis and specifically points to BTC.D reclaim as potentially more valuable than larger composites, but those retrospective numerical results still require independent reproduction.

### ETF-era hidden deterioration

BTC can remain structurally healthy while ecosystem transmission fails. This is one of the strongest cross-archive findings and should remain a dedicated research family.

### Stress / flush

CFGI, ETF flows, liquidation clusters, OI/funding and reclaim quality appear to perform different diagnostic jobs. The value is likely in functional decomposition and timing, not in treating any single stress input as a bottom predictor.

### Opportunity cost

The framework historically instrumented false positives better than false negatives. FNP / Cumulative FNP is therefore essential to determine whether extra confirmations actually improve risk-adjusted outcomes or merely make the framework right too late.

## What should continue to be observed

- ETH/BTC persistence
- breadth survival
- BTC.D reclaim / acceleration / rollover
- ETF flow persistence, concentration and absorption quality
- stablecoin deployment rather than supply alone
- hidden deterioration under stable BTC
- OI/funding/liquidation reset and reclaim quality
- sequence survival and decay
- FNP / opportunity cost

## What should be treated skeptically or ignored unless recovered

- exact legacy formulas for LCT/EPE/CRL/SIM/SSE where source definitions remain unavailable
- old percentage claims without rows
- large composite scores that cannot demonstrate incremental value
- historical attribution to SRE/FAE where contemporaneous inputs are missing
- automatic RWE weighting from historical fit

## Highest-value next research

Run a sensor-combination tournament on the best preserved event windows:

- single-family baselines
- two-family combinations
- three-family combinations
- full shadow composite
- current stack where comparable

Judge by false positives, false negatives, lead-time, regime robustness, calibration and complexity penalty.

The key question is no longer `which old sensor sounds smartest?`

It is:

> What is the smallest independent set of information that gives the framework the earliest reliable view of rotation, fake rotation, pullback quality, hidden deterioration and distribution?
