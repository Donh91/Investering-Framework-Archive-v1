# Executive Verdict

## Bottom line

The historical Shadow Layer contains real research value, but the value is concentrated in a smaller set of information families than the number of named sensors suggests.

The strongest recurring information families are:

1. **ETH/BTC persistence / relative leadership**
2. **Breadth survival / participation durability**
3. **BTC dominance path and reclaim / rollover behavior**
4. **Flow quality versus mere flow quantity, especially ETF-era absorption versus ecosystem transmission**
5. **Leverage / liquidation / post-flush structure as timing and exhaustion context**
6. **Sequence persistence and failure, rather than first signal alone**
7. **False-negative / opportunity-cost accounting as a governance control**

The weakest area is not lack of ideas. It is historical identity and clean event-level provenance. Many old named sensors were described, integrated or discussed without enough immutable contemporaneous rows to support a historical hit-rate claim.

## The most important correction to prior Stage 2 scope

The 14 recovered legacy sensors are only one branch of the Shadow Layer. The actual shadow universe also includes:

- Entry Signal Ledger family
- legacy forward-test family
- breadth-forward family
- Fable/OTA shadow research
- Cycle Navigator research
- Historical Altseason Pullback Lab
- ETF-era quality engines and meta-scores
- derivatives/options shadow
- stress/flush diagnostics
- risk/exit shadow components
- macro/liquidity overlays
- false-negative and adaptive-learning layers

The Shadow Registry therefore must be treated as the population anchor, with archaeology extending beyond currently registered names.

## Strongest historical learning

### 1. Persistence beats spikes

This principle appears repeatedly across the archive and is the most coherent cross-generation learning. ETH/BTC spikes, BTC.D dips, ETF inflow days, stablecoin growth and isolated breadth attempts repeatedly proved too easy to misread when persistence was absent.

Verdict: `KEEP` as architecture-level research principle.

### 2. BTC health is not ecosystem health

ETF-era research consistently separates BTC absorption from broader ecosystem transmission. This is one of the most useful conceptual upgrades because it explains why BTC can remain structurally healthy while ETH/BTC, breadth and alts deteriorate.

Verdict: `KEEP` as regime-classification information family.

### 3. ETH/BTC + breadth + BTC.D appear to contain much of the discriminative rotation information

The historical F12.5 replay material reported that its strongest discrimination came from BTC.D reclaim, ETH/BTC persistence and breadth survival. The same replay described the 10-day BTC.D reclaim rule as the strongest individual component. Because that replay is retrospective and not fully independently reproduced here, its numbers are not promoted to fact. However, it materially strengthens the hypothesis that some later composite scores may be redundant transformations of a smaller core.

Verdict: `KEEP` for the simple components, `REDUNDANCY_TEST_REQUIRED` for larger composites.

### 4. Old hit-rate claims are frequently weaker than their wording suggested

Examples:

- Shadow v1-v8 explicitly called its hit-rate table a synthesis rather than a statistical backtest.
- Early Rotation Pre-Trigger was later described with very high historical reliability claims despite small sample and survivorship risk.
- Fake Rotation Type 3's approximately 55-75% failure claim was not reproduced by the independent recovery review.

Verdict: historical percentage claims without event rows must be treated as `UNVERIFIED_ARCHIVE_CLAIM`.

### 5. The framework historically measured false positives better than false negatives

Claude Research Lab material identified a structural asymmetry: the framework was highly instrumented for avoiding bad entries, but much less instrumented for measuring the cost of waiting too long. This led to the False Negative / Cumulative FNP concept and the observation that a well-governed framework can still be right too late.

Verdict: `KEEP` as governance/meta-learning control. This is not a market sensor but is essential to evaluating sensor usefulness honestly.

## What currently has actual repository-native forward evidence?

The Entry Signal Ledger is the clearest current example, but sample size is still tiny. Current performance summary shows two activation events, only one matured 24h event, and no matured 7d/14d/30d event set. The single matured 24h observation was positive for both BTC and ETH, but this is not enough to estimate edge.

Verdict: `WATCH`, not validated.

## What should not be promoted from this review

No historical result in this review authorizes:

- automatic live weighting,
- new market thresholds,
- portfolio execution,
- canonical rule changes,
- direct activation of RWE or other adaptive weighting,
- revival of old fixed BTC.D or ETH/BTC thresholds merely because they appear in archived documents.

## Best current research direction

The highest-value next research is not to add more sensors. It is to run a **sensor-combination and sequence tournament** using the best preserved contemporaneous data, asking whether a small set of independent information families matches or exceeds the larger shadow stack.

Priority hypotheses:

1. `ETHBTC_PERSISTENCE + BREADTH_SURVIVAL + BTCD_PATH` captures most rotation discrimination.
2. `ETF_FLOW_QUALITY + BTCD + ETHBTC + BREADTH` distinguishes BTC absorption from ecosystem deterioration better than ETF flows alone.
3. `LIQUIDATION_CLEARANCE + OI/FUNDING_RESET + RECLAIM_QUALITY` is useful primarily as a timing/exhaustion classifier, not a directional predictor.
4. `FNP / opportunity-cost accounting` will identify cases where additional confirmations add little protection but meaningful delay.
5. Several meta-scores, especially TPS/DRS-style composites, may be useful summaries but may not add independent information after their components are known.

## Final scientific stance

The archive supports a meaningful Shadow Layer research program, but not a claim that dozens of named shadow sensors each possess separate edge.

The likely truth is simpler:

> A small number of durable information families are repeatedly viewed through many transformations. The research task is to identify which transformations improve timing or classification and which merely create the appearance of multiple confirmations.
