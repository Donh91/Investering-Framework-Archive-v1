# Gaps, Falsification and Next Research

## Gap classification

Every unresolved historical question belongs to one of five buckets:

- `GITHUB_CAN_RESOLVE`
- `ARCHIVE_PACKET_CAN_RESOLVE`
- `EXTERNAL_RECOVERY_JUSTIFIED`
- `DEFINITION_RECOVERY_REQUIRED`
- `UNTESTABLE`

## Highest-priority unresolved questions

### 1. Can the simple rotation triad beat larger composites?

Hypothesis:

`ETHBTC_PERSISTENCE + BREADTH_SURVIVAL + BTCD_PATH`

captures most of the discrimination of TPS/DRS/rotation-readiness style composites.

Why this matters:

If true, the framework can become simpler and less vulnerable to pseudo-independent confirmations.

Falsification:

The full composite must show stable incremental performance after complexity penalty and across regimes.

Status: `GITHUB_CAN_RESOLVE` partially, depending historical breadth/BTC.D continuity.

### 2. Does ETF-flow quality add value beyond flow sign?

Hypothesis:

Persistence/concentration/absorption quality explains more than raw positive/negative ETF flow.

Falsification:

Raw 5d/10d flow sign performs equally well across hidden-deterioration and pullback outcomes.

Status: `GITHUB_CAN_RESOLVE` for modern ETF era.

### 3. Does hidden deterioration lead alt weakness while BTC remains healthy?

Hypothesis:

Weakening ETH/BTC + breadth + deployment under stable BTC identifies portfolio-health deterioration before BTC price deterioration.

Falsification:

The combination does not outperform ETH/BTC or breadth alone, or produces too many false alarms in healthy BTC-led expansions.

Status: `GITHUB_CAN_RESOLVE` for modern period, historical extension may require external recovery.

### 4. Is Fake Rotation Type 3 useful as veto after removing the old failure-rate claim?

Hypothesis:

The qualitative pattern remains useful even though the 55-75% rate was not reproduced.

Falsification:

High BTC.D + weak breadth alone matches the full Type 3 classifier.

Status: `DEFINITION_RECOVERY_REQUIRED` plus modern event rows.

### 5. Does Early Rotation Pre-Trigger provide incremental lead-time?

Hypothesis:

The composite detects early transmission before classic ETH/BTC/breadth confirmation.

Falsification:

Its lead comes entirely from one component, or false starts offset the timing advantage.

Status: historical exact test often `UNTESTABLE`; prospective data readiness remains relevant only after historical mining is exhausted.

### 6. Are stress/flush inputs directional or merely diagnostic?

Hypothesis:

Liquidation clearance + OI/funding reset + reclaim quality improves exhaustion timing.

Falsification:

The stack only describes completed selloffs and fails to improve post-event path classification.

Status: `GITHUB_CAN_RESOLVE` on modern events when enough cases mature.

### 7. How expensive is confirmation delay?

Hypothesis:

Several gates add meaningful false-negative cost without equivalent false-positive reduction in fast recovery regimes.

Falsification:

Delayed permission consistently avoids enough subsequent drawdown to compensate for missed upside.

Status: `ARCHIVE_PACKET_CAN_RESOLVE` partially via FNP/decision ledgers; more rows needed.

## Negative controls required

For each sufficiently testable sensor family:

- matched non-trigger periods
- timestamp-shift placebo
- component-only baseline
- ETH/BTC-only baseline
- BTC.D-only baseline
- breadth-only baseline
- simple combined baseline
- current-stack state over the same window
- source-version split
- regime split

## Kill criteria

A shadow sensor should enter retirement research if one or more apply:

1. exact definition cannot be recovered and no modern equivalent is justified;
2. it is largely deterministic transformation of a simpler retained input;
3. it fails to add lead-time, calibration or veto precision;
4. it only works after outcome-informed threshold selection;
5. it cannot outperform matched placebo or simple baseline;
6. source cost / fragility is disproportionate to incremental value;
7. it duplicates a stronger sensor family with worse provenance.

## Deep Research queue

Deep Research should be invoked only after exact missing timestamps / variables are known.

Priority external recovery candidates:

1. historical BTC.D with frozen convention for specific replay windows;
2. historical breadth for specific rotation/fake-rotation events;
3. stablecoin deployment proxies for preregistered ETF-era windows;
4. missing CFGI values only where sentiment is a pre-specified sensor component;
5. options/liquidation history only where it can change a stress/flush conclusion.

Do not launch broad internet research to recreate the framework from scratch.

## Research completion standard

The historical program is considered mature when:

- all named shadow families are either mapped, retired or explicitly untestable;
- every retained family has a defined information role;
- simple baselines have been tested wherever data allows;
- redundancy clusters are documented;
- old percentage claims are either reproduced or retired;
- false-negative cost is measured alongside false positives;
- no historical result directly changes canonical authority.
