## Executive finding

**Verdict: CYCLE_CLOCK_UNPROVEN_AS_INCREMENTAL_FORECAST_FEATURE; REGIME_TRANSITION_STUDY_JUSTIFIED.**

The occurrence of an October 2025 high and a major November 2026 drawdown is *compatible* with a historical roughly four-year rhythm. Compatibility is not proof of independent predictive information, a bottom, a 2027 all-time-high date, or an actionable new allocation rule. Bitcoin's maturation, global financial conditions, ETF participation, leverage and risk appetite may generate some of the same timing associations. The decisive comparison is not whether a historical cycle diagram looks right; it is whether a preregistered calendar-phase feature improves identical-clock outcomes over official Kompas, price-only and regime-aware competitors, after leakage, clustering, multiple testing and source costs.

**Primary actionable research value:** treat cycle phase as an *unpromoted conditional context hypothesis*, and study earlier recognition of **STRESS/DELEVERAGING -> FRAGILE_RECOVERY -> DURABLE_BTC_RECOVERY -> BROAD_EXPANSION**, while never conflating BTC demand with ecosystem-wide rotation. These names describe hypothetical test strata, not additional live market states.

**Production decision:** NO CHANGE. No bottom or top call. Do not translate an X chart, Ichimoku signal, liquidation map or Wyckoff color into NOW/RISK_DOWN/BUY authority.

## 3. Scientific hypotheses and critical controls

**H0, no incremental cycle edge:** Once identical-clock price, trend, volatility and existing regime descriptors are included, 'time since halving' does not improve relevant next-horizon forecast loss. This is the reference null, not a claim that cyclicality does not exist.

**H1, calendar increment:** Continuous days-since-halving or pre-specified 4-year circular phase contributes incremental information, beyond price-only and today's official Kompas. All feature transforms and period length fixed before evaluation; do not select top/bottom windows from future extrema.

**H2, conditional regime interpretation:** The cycle feature only helps identify *when* an already-existing stress/recovery transition becomes more probable, after controlling real-time ETF flows, macro vintages, market breadth and leverage. This is an interaction question and a high-multiplicity risk, not a guaranteed accuracy gain.

**H3, no unique 4-year mechanism:** Similar value appears for shifted calendar clocks, arbitrary 3.5/4.5-year phases, reverse phase, or equally flexible price-trend analogs. If so, the special halving interpretation fails regardless of chart fit.

**H4, economic relevance:** A better regime probability need not improve actionable warnings or deployment. Conditional false-alarm, missed-recovery, lead-time and asymmetric opportunity-cost evaluations require an independently eligible decision ledger; without it report NOT_EVALUABLE, never hypothetical PnL.

### Minimum credible comparison, only if an existing original-owner admissibility gate opens

- **Arm P:** exact same-clock price-only forecast / volatility persistence baseline.
- **Arm I:** current official issued Kompas and its independently frozen/available forecast, with abstentions preserved.
- **Arm C:** Arm P plus predeclared calendar phase, *no other added parameters*.
- **Arm R:** Arm P plus currently available regime indicators, using existing production/research source definitions.
- **Arm R+C:** regime indicators plus calendar phase, assessed strictly as an *ablation* against R with identical clocks, observations and source coverage. No model selection based on current November stress.

This is an **analysis blueprint**, not an active preregistration or authorized experiment. Defer creation of any new candidate if the current MAR, edge ledger or Task 2 owners can directly accommodate an equivalent feature ablation. Avoid inflating effective N by treating days, BTC/ETH assets, overlapping horizons or correlated events as independent.

### Test identities and required admission

- Historical admissible development data only where point-in-time original first-known values can be proven. No Round 3 hypothesis testing, tuning, outcome linkage or scoring.
- Freeze sample inclusion, timestamped source availability, lag rules, target horizon, feature formulas, all baseline issuance clocks, missingness and event clustering **before** looking at relevant outcomes. Historical 2026-11 stress selected because of this research discussion is **development/selection-exposed**, not an untouched confirmatory holdout.
- Use calendar phase and exogenous rule timestamps known on the issue date. Never align to future-known price top/bottom. No hindsight Wyckoff or shifted-forward Ichimoku cloud point.
- Record per-arm forecast identity, model/contract commit/blob, input data SHA, exchange/instrument/quote, UTC bar **close**, vendor first-known time, producer issue, first durable publication, source-valid-until and consumer first-access. Reject mismatched target clocks.
- Compare like-for-like outputs: directional hit-rate on common directional targets; probabilistic Brier/log loss only for genuine pre-issued probabilities; interval coverage/sharpness only against specified interval semantics; cost only with true decisions/fills. Do not label a non-nominal CN range as an 80% probabilistic interval.
- Chronological expanding walk-forward, purge/embargo for overlapping horizons, grouped events, separate pre/post-Jan-2024 ETF-era and liquidity-regime strata, leave-one-era-out where meaningful. Report eligibility N and effective independent event N separately; **tiny sample => INSUFFICIENT_EVIDENCE**, not significance by bootstrap.
- Calendar placebo: phase shifted by plus/minus 180 days and alternative cycle lengths fixed a priori. Additional falsifier: trend/momentum equivalent with same feature count. If shifted placebo is equivalent, unique four-year interpretation loses support.
- Multiple-testing control across arms, horizons, regimes and time shifts; no cherry-picked 'best 2026 bottom' specification.
- Negative cases: missing ETF settlement/availability, absent macro vintage, stale public site output, opposite ETH/BTC/breadth, rebound without durability, news exogenous shock, holiday/weekend flow closure, label lookahead, expected-liquidation-map mistaken for realized events.
- A promotion gate requires preregistered MDE, independent review, eligible forward evidence and a reversible owner-approved integration path. This note supplies **no new numeric success threshold**.

## 4. Practical possible learning for Regime and Kompas, without changing authority

1. **Separate descriptive cycle context from live tactical regime.** A 4-year phase is a slow calendar prior, not a contemporaneous stress measurement and not an actionable low.
2. **Transition evidence > snapshot storytelling.** Research a fixed chronological sequence: forced selling / OI reduction / spot-vs-ETF absorption / price stabilization / 1-3d trend recovery / multiweek survival / ETH and breadth transmission. Each observation must be source-backed with exact clocks; no unearned inference of causal whales or retail from order-size bars.
3. **BTC health != alt ecosystem health.** Long-horizon BTC rebound can coexist with poor ETH/BTC and weak breadth. Rotation is separately gated.
4. **Negative learning is an outcome.** If calendar phase fails after price/macro controls, retain an explicit null result and suppress decorative cycle inputs; do not tune until fit appears.
5. **Protection and opportunity costs should be paired.** Early-risk false positives, warning lead time and missed recoveries may have different optimal horizons, but the data must show the actual trade-off before policy change.
6. **No public-facing invented certainty.** The future Kompas may *explain* cycle context as a narrative qualifier if separately reviewed, but must not convert e.g. Chifoi 51k or Galaxy 40-46k into a prediction band, guarantee, countdown or probability.
