## 1. Extract precisely what the author says — not our extrapolation
The X post has five numbered sections.

1. **Stay optimistic/flexible and obey strategy-specific rules.** Buy-and-hold / DCA and trend-following have different correct actions. As a US growth-stock trend follower, author *personally* shifts toward mostly cash with breaks below **21-day EMA, 50-day SMA**, failed breakouts, leadership deterioration and distribution signs. This is not a proof that both moving averages are calibrated crypto SELL thresholds. Recognize reversals and follow-through days; maintain a buy list, not an indiscriminate dip-buy.
2. **Protect psychological and monetary capital.** Avoid many small whipsaw losses in regime-incompatible choppy markets; specialize rather than switching strategies merely to stay active. The author's references to QQQ and IWO, CANSLIM and fund ownership are **equity-market-specific**.
3. **Identify new leaders DURING the correction.** Seek rising **Relative Strength (RS) line** against index while market/benchmark is down, especially new RS highs before price highs, orderly base/right-side construction, persistent relative resilience. The “beach ball underwater” and assumed institutional accumulation are *interpretations/hypotheses*, not independently observed custody or flow evidence.
4. **Study.** Research past bases, earnings gap-ups, breakout structure and actual winners/losers. Lists multiple classic trading books and TraderLion resources; book links are recommendations, not primary evidence of an outcome or algorithm.
5. **Rest and refrain from forced action.** Preserve capacity to act when the market presents a compatible opportunity. No claim that inactivity earns positive alpha.

**Framing fidelity:** Moglen is describing discretionary US equity growth momentum, primarily daily chart horizons, NOT a mechanical 12-hour BTC/ETH probability model, a 1–3-day token moonshot strategy, a crypto perpetuals system, or a causal proof of institution buys. Examples such as Zoom after March 2020 are **selected anecdotes**, not a survivor-adjusted experimental denominator. “The deeper the correction, the better” is motivational context and **NOT** a justified instruction to use leverage or to buy bigger after declines.

## 2. Clarify the exact quantitative object
- **RS line ≠ RSI.** RS line for candidate i relative to BTC = `RS_i(t) = adjusted_price_i(t) / price_BTC(t)`, same quote currency and same completed timestamp, with rebasing at universe admission where justified. An upward RS line can occur because candidate falls **less**, even if its own absolute trend is still negative. Study `log(P_i(t)/P_i(t-k)) - log(P_BTC(t)/P_BTC(t-k))` with fixed lookbacks; no hidden intraday unfinished bars.
- **RS new high before price (RSNHBP):** preregister as-of whether RS_i breaks its prior fixed window high while absolute price_i has not regained its same-window high. Must disclose exact high window and survivor/universe membership; do not optimize period after seeing winning coins.
- **Stress resilience vs low beta:** rank raw relative gain *and* volatility/beta-normalized residual, **plus negative controls with similarly low-beta assets**. “Fell less” may simply mean illiquidity, stale quotes or low beta, not future leadership. Exclude stablecoins/wrapped assets via vintage membership rules; report stale/delisted and unavailable tokens, do not choose from today's survivors.
- **Follow-through vs false rebound:** Moglen's US market volume confirmation and 21/50-day conventions require adaptation to 24/7 BTC/crypto closing calendar, 7-day volume seasonality, derivatives and spot breadth. Do not import Nasdaq/QQQ FTD thresholds verbatim as valid crypto signal rules.
- **Leadership ≠ ecosystem rotation:** isolated strength of five alts is not proof of broad altseason; align cohort breadth, BTC/ETH, ETH/BTC and capital transmission. Do not infer a causal institution actor from an RS line.

## 4. Prioritized falsifiable hypotheses — existing #209 only
`H-MOG-01 / UNDERWATER_LEADERSHIP`:
Among **all** eligible liquid candidates frozen before/during an already-defined BTC correction, do assets with (a) relative-strength improvement while BTC weakens and (b) genuinely positive absolute price/breadth participation produce higher **next 7d/14d/30d risk-adjusted realizable** returns, winner recall or earlier reliable leader identification than same-clock raw 7d/30d momentum, simple past drawdown/risk, beta, volume and fixed-cap universe? Use single held-out prospective survivor family, not cherry-picked Zoom/altseason 2020. Missing and delisted symbols count in denominator.

`H-MOG-02 / BREAKOUT_FAILURE_AND_TREND_STRESS`:
Can predeclared counts of (a) candidate breakout attempts then prompt invalidation, (b) participation-concentration deterioration and (c) BTC/ETH price versus 21-day EMA and 50-day SMA provide incremental **1d/3d/7d warning lead** over existing Official+Shadow and a **simple price-only** champion, *without* simply raising all risk warnings to 4/5? `H-MOG-03 / REENTRY_WITHOUT_OPPORTUNITY_COST_TRAP`:

## 5. Exact minimal research protocol / pre-evidence contract
**Source clock and universe:**
- Cohort entry by original timestamped available daily snapshot; fix 00:00 UTC close and complete-bar policy for crypto **before sampling**. Chart assets and all venues must use same relevant timestamp; no Monday weekend-free US market shortcut.
- Universe: as-of top-100 market-cap cohort where original membership/price observations are available; exclude stablecoins, wrapped tokens and synthetic mirrors with a versioned register; capture delisted/absent coins rather than replacing with current popular names. No present-day ranking used for past selections.
- Distinguish source `price_date`, `candle_close_utc`, `first_known_at_utc`, `first_publication_at`, `retrieval_utc`, `eligible_at_utc`, source hash, instrument identity, liquidity/notional, and completeness. Original issuer vintage is mandatory for hindsight-free scoring. Unconfirmed historical archive can be used only to design method, never get original issued forecast credit.
- Regime event: existing official correction/rebound owners first. For a simple benchmark, consider a fixed BTC prior-window drawdown and 21EMA/50SMA cross evaluated after completed candle; EXACT numeric drawdown cutoff, lookback and independent shock cluster policy must be **pre-frozen before any evaluation**. Do not choose thresholds by fitting the current Oct 2026 selloff. Declare whether “BTC correction” means spot BTC, TOTAL3 or another cohort, and why.
- Rank `log(P_i/P_BTC)` slope and new-high-before-price flags with **fixed** 7d/21d/30d candidate windows selected a priori as a *small multi-testing family*. Test absolute trend and low-beta/momentum baselines; do not adjust high windows to pick winners.
- Events/outcomes: sample all states including no candidate, all breakouts including failures, all leaders that later fail, and all nonleaders that later become leaders. Count correlated shock-hours as **one** family for uncertainty. Horizons +24h/+72h/+7d/+14d/+30d; due-only outcomes by existing ledger. All censored/unknown kept.
- Complexity: compare **B0** BTC spot price-only 21EMA/50SMA/no trade; **B1** existing ETH/BTC + breadth + RQS current as-of (only when actually available); **B2** raw cross-sectional price momentum and low-beta control; **B3** B1 + frozen RS-survivor feature. No new favorite strategy added after opening outcomes. No “we predicted rebound” from a retrospective price chart.
- Negative controls: same market shock but random candidate rank; sign-permuted time; lagging/hindsight RS values; low beta defensive and stale assets; cap/liq matched non-survivors; equally liquid coins not ranked; fake breakout; delayed entry by 1 daily bar. A challenger that only identifies low beta and illiquid stale quotes must be rejected.
- Score: precision/recall of real next leaders **at fixed population rate**; leader survival/false leader; median and tail returns and max drawdown; lead time before index recovery; miss rate (false-negative opportunity cost); breadth-aware front-loaded risk warning false alarm rate; after-cost net wealth, volatility and feasible execution. Use cluster bootstrap; family multiplicity correction; walk-forward, leave-one-regime-out, independent review. Parameter tuning on available historic observations = DEVELOPMENT, not independent OOS.
- `UNKNOWN` for missing original price/universe, source lag, inability to simulate executable trades, restricted origin, uncontrolled paper-trade fees, skipped reentry or unverified issuance. Do not score as negative or positive.
- Trading status: **NOTHING IS PROMOTED** to live warning 4/5, Kompas 12h/1–3d, CN Monday forecasts, BUY/SELL/rebuy, risk exposure or portfolio. Any eventual Shadow candidate goes through original #1596 and #209 review/promotion owners.

## 6. External corroboration and adversarial literature

Across 58 conventional liquid futures, 1–12-month time-series momentum; does NOT validate 21d EMA crypto alpha, cross-sectional RS leader selection, or 12h BTC forecast.

**Crypto-specific scientific constraints:**
- Fieberg, Liedtke & Zaremba, *Cryptocurrency anomalies and economic constraints*, *International Review of Financial Analysis* 94, 2024, https://doi.org/10.1016/j.irfa.2024.103218 : gross momentum/size/volume patterns often suffer high trading costs, illiquidity and bull-regime concentration; therefore after-cost and eligible-universe controls central.
- *State transitions and momentum effect in cryptocurrency market*, *Finance Research Letters* 86, Dec 2025, https://doi.org/10.1016/j.frl.2025.108356 : authors find crypto momentum concentrated in sustained UP→UP states versus down or transition; this is a direct **falsifier** for assuming RS during a correction earns immediate profits.
- *Momentum in the Cryptocurrency Market: A Comprehensive Analysis under Realistic Assumptions*, *Review of Asset Pricing Studies*, accepted manuscript visible Oct 2026, https://academic.oup.com/raps/advance-article-abstract/doi/10.1093/rapstu/raag011/8914819 : stronger time-series than cross-sectional momentum evidence under realistic liquidation/fee assumptions, specific methodology/estimates must be checked before extrapolation.
- Original Research Lab prior [Residual Momentum Binance proxy 2026-10-29](https://github.com/Donh91/Investering-Framework-Archive-v1/issues/1512#issuecomment-6483269314) : 12 weeks, top-5 relative selection did **not** beat its simpler controls, with source/venue/market sample caveats; different population and timescale so not universal rejection of H-MOG-01.
