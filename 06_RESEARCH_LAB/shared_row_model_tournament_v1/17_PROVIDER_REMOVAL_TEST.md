# Provider / Version Robustness

For every family with multiple legitimate providers, the tournament will rerun after removing or substituting one provider at a time without changing transforms. A candidate fails provider robustness if its apparent edge disappears solely because one fragile provider/version is present.

No provider substitution may silently alter denominator, universe, timestamp convention or revision status.

Status: `FROZEN_PENDING_ROWS_AND_OWNER_BINDINGS`.
