# Regime Sensitivity

The historical catalog contains 45 pullback episodes in the 2020-21 research window and one modern analogue episode in 2025. This is not enough to claim historical post-ETF robustness for the candidate tournament.

Pre-ETF and post-ETF results must therefore be reported separately. Pooled performance may not hide a regime failure. Prospective rows are the primary source for ETF-era comparative evidence.
