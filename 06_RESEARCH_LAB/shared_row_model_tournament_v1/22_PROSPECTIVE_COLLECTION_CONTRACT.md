# Prospective Collection Contract

Status: `ACTIVATED_RESEARCH_ONLY`.

A row becomes eligible only after every family used by that candidate has a frozen owner, timestamp convention, transform version and missing/stale policy. Candidate decisions are generated at the observation cutoff and serialized before outcome maturity.

Outcomes mature at exactly 24h, 72h and 7d. Candidate disagreements are copied immediately to the FNP ledger and cannot be edited except to append matured outcomes.

Catalyst evidence is frozen at row creation. Historical reconstruction is forbidden where source identity or semantics are unresolved.

The collector has no canonical market or portfolio authority.
