# Prospective Family Freeze Queue

Authority: `RESEARCH_ONLY_NON_CANONICAL`.

This queue is deliberately adversarial. A lane becomes `READY` only when owner identity, source/version convention, transform semantics, information-cutoff behavior and candidate-decision mapping are reproducible before outcomes.

## P0 - unlock the core tournament

1. `ETHBTC_PERSISTENCE`: source + transform are reproduced. Freeze the tournament decision mapping without changing the existing 0.0300 persistence contract.
2. `BREADTH_SURVIVAL`: preregister a prospective-only transform from the frozen Top100 membership/aggregate owner. Do not claim it is recovered legacy semantics unless quantitative mapping is actually found.
3. `BTCD_PATH_RECLAIM`: keep `CMC_DIRECT_SOURCE_CONVENTION` explicit, persist it daily, and preregister the path/reclaim transform. Never relabel CMC as TradingView equivalence.
4. Freeze candidate decision contracts for singles, pairs and `C07_SIMPLE_3` only after all three are READY.

## P1 - incremental challengers

- `ETF_FLOW_QUALITY`: recover or preregister exact quality/absorption semantics from finalized Farside owner rows.
- `LEVERAGE_LIQUIDATION_RECLAIM`: acquire legitimate liquidation/reclaim evidence or remain blocked. OI/funding/taker are not substitutes.
- `FULL_STACK_REPRODUCIBILITY`: freeze the row-time family/version map and candidate decision contract.

## P2 - optional challengers

- `STABLECOIN_DEPLOYMENT_PARKING`: separate deployment/parking from supply/liquidity.
- `CFGI_TIMING_CONTEXT`: restore exact CFGI source/engine/version coverage. Alternative.me substitution is forbidden.

Dynamic candidates `C10_STABLECOIN_CHALLENGER`, `C11_CFGI_CHALLENGER` and `C12_FULL_STACK` require their own frozen base/dynamic decision contracts. `BEST_SPARSE_AT_EVALUATION_TIME` and Full Stack decisions must never be inferred by the controller.

Raw evidence captured before a transform freeze may be retained for source QA and forward availability studies. It may not receive retrospective candidate decisions after outcomes are known.
