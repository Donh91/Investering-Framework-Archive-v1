# Non-Inferiority Sensitivity

These are research evaluation tolerances, not market thresholds.

## Strict
0 pp tolerated FPR deterioration, 0 pp FNR deterioration, 0h lead-time loss, no robustness loss, no unique failure-mode loss.

## Moderate grid
FPR deterioration: 1 / 2 / 5 pp.
FNR deterioration: 1 / 2 / 5 pp.
Lead-time loss: 6 / 12 / 24h.
Complexity reduction must be at least 30 percent and no unique failure mode may be lost.

## Relaxed task-bounded grid
FPR/FNR deterioration: 5 / 10 pp.
Lead-time loss: 24 / 48h.
Only a named bounded task may use this conclusion.

A minimal-stack verdict is emitted only after all relevant cells have measured shared-row evidence.
