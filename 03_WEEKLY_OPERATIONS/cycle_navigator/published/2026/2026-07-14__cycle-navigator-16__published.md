## 🧭 WEEKLY MARKET UPDATE

### Last Week’s Thesis

Last week tested whether BTC could absorb pressure around 61.9K without breaking the broader repair structure.

It did.

BTC survived the support test and recovered toward the upper part of its range. ETH/BTC also maintained its repair above 0.0275.

### What the Market Proved

The latest flush did not destroy the repair.

BTC absorbed selling pressure without a structural breakdown, while ETH continued improving relative to BTC.

That reduced the probability of an immediate return to acute stress.

### What It Failed to Prove

The recovery did not become broad or durable.

Short-term strength improved, but capital remained concentrated in BTC and ETH instead of spreading through the wider risk curve.

The market repaired **vertically through the leaders**, but not yet **horizontally across the market**.

### How the Map Changed

Immediate breakdown risk decreased.

But the probability of moving directly into broad expansion did not rise equally.

The market has entered a narrower confirmation zone where holding support alone is no longer enough.

### This Week’s Test

This week is about **durability and transmission**.

BTC must convert the reclaim area into support. ETH/BTC must preserve relative strength. Breadth and flows must show that repair can spread beyond the leaders.

### Weekly Compass

**Last week proved that repair can survive.**  
**This week must prove that repair can transmit.**
