## 1. Active architecture

The active framework is compressed to these layers:

```text
1. DATA PING truth layer
2. Main-framework judgment and permission layer
3. Shadow / Research Lab challenger layer
4. Immutable accountability ledgers
5. Master Monday weekly ratification
6. Cycle Navigator public communication
7. GitHub archive and governance control plane
```

No other named layer has independent authority unless it is explicitly listed in the Rule and Evidence Registry.

## 2. Five diagnostic families

### A. Macro Context

Includes:

- TechDev roadmap and business-cycle context;
- FRED;
- ISM;
- Copper/Gold;
- macro liquidity and external shock context.

Decision effect: readiness and scenario weighting only. No standalone deploy authority.

### B. Flow and Absorption

Includes:

- ETF print versus trend;
- stablecoin supply and deployment;
- spot quality;
- liquidity parking versus transmission;
- absorption quality.

Decision effect: urgency, recovery quality and permission confidence.

### C. Transmission and Rotation

Includes:

- ETH/BTC;
- BTC dominance;
- breadth;
- deployment;
- large/mid/small/micro participation;
- flow congruence.

Decision effect: alt-lane permission and stage transition evidence.

### D. Stress and Structure

Includes:

- price and volatility;
- funding and OI;
- liquidation clusters;
- gamma/VRP when available;
- pullback, flush and post-flush path;
- reclaim and deterioration gates.

Decision effect: risk warning, survival, recovery and capital protection review.

### E. Accountability and Scoring

Includes:

- Forecast Ledger;
- Sequence Ledger;
- FNP;
- event calibration;
- baselines;
- kill monitor;
- source lineage.

Decision effect: rule survival, promotion, suspension or death.

## 5. Capital-state to public-phase crosswalk

| Internal capital state | Public phase | BTC permission | ETH / large-cap permission | Mid/small/micro permission | Meaning |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| WAIT | Pre-Rotation / BTC Dominance Expansion | Hold/watch; BTC-partial only inside explicit forward test | Wait/watch | No deploy | Evidence insufficient or defensive state active |
| PREPARE | Early Rotation Watch | Partial BTC test may be evaluated; no automatic live unlock | Watchlist / conditional preparation | Watch only | Next-stage evidence is forming but not survived |
| DEPLOY-1 | Selective Alt Rotation | BTC allowed by framework; asset-specific sizing | Selective ETH/large-cap permission | Limited and only with transmission | Narrow rotation has survived initial gates |
| DEPLOY-2 | Broad Altseason | Staged exposure | Broad large-cap and selected mid-cap permission | Broader staged permission; micro remains highest bar | Multi-axis transmission and breadth confirmed |
| PROTECT | Late Cycle / Exit | Trim/protect according to state | Trim faster as quality deteriorates | Small/micro protected first | Distribution or late-cycle risk dominates |
| POST_ROTATION | Post-Rotation / Reset | Reassess core | Locked until reset evidence | Locked | Prior rotation ended; new sequence required |

This table is a translation, not a new engine.

## 6. BTC lane versus alt lane

```text
BTC_LANE:
  may use BTC structure, macro shift, ETF/spot absorption and hard-death survival
  may be forward-tested before ecosystem rotation confirms

ALT_LANE:
  requires ETHBTC, breadth, BTC.D, deployment and flow congruence
  stricter survival requirement

MICROCAP_LANE:
  highest permission threshold
  microcap-only pumps never prove rotation
```

BTC health is not ecosystem health. Ecosystem weakness does not automatically erase a BTC-specific test, and BTC strength does not automatically unlock alts.

## 8. Cycle Navigator display contract

Cycle Navigator should show:

- the current public phase;
- the next conditional phase;
- measurable unlocks;
- invalidation;
- forward-test status;
- separate range, phase and rotation accountability.

Specific calendar countdowns are allowed only when frozen as forecasts. Otherwise use conditional windows and state-based language.
