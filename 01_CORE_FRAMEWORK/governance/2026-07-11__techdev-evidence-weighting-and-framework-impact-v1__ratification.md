## Calibration conclusion

TechDev remains valuable, but its value is asymmetric:

- Higher value in broad regime awareness, research synthesis and scenario generation.
- Lower value in exact timing, price targets and repeated “imminent” rotation windows.
- Material governance risk when analog flexibility, correlated confluence or moved invalidations create excessive confidence.
- No evidence supports standalone execution authority.

No current market state, gate, lock or portfolio action is changed by this historical ratification.
