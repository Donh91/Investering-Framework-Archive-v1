## 🧭 CURRENT MARKET STATE

BTC is now trading around $80K, ETH around $2.5K, and ETH/BTC remains above the important 0.0300 structural level.

That is meaningful progress, but it does not mean broad altseason has arrived.

The latest Top-100 breadth proxy recovered to roughly 73% advancers. At the same time, only around 30% of the Top-100 are outperforming BTC, while BTC dominance remains close to 59%.

So participation is broadening, but BTC is still absorbing a large share of the move.

This is why the framework separates four stages:

**Leadership → Transmission → Participation → Survival**

ETH/BTC tells us leadership has improved.

Breadth tells us participation is beginning to expand.

BTC dominance helps tell us whether capital is actually transmitting through the rest of the market.

The main question is therefore no longer whether rotation can begin.

It is whether rotation can survive and propagate.

## ⏳ COUNTDOWN TO BROAD ALTSEASON

This is not a prediction that broad altseason suddenly starts next week.

The framework currently sees several stages developing before sustained broad participation is likely.

The working timeline assumes the present rotation survives and capital continues moving down the market-cap structure.

| Phase | Working timeline |
|---|---|
| ETH / large-cap rotation | Now |
| Mid-cap expansion | Now to ~2 weeks |
| Small-cap transmission | ~1-3 weeks |
| Microcap participation | ~2-4 weeks |
| Broad altcoin season | ~2-6 weeks |
| Broad altseason mania | ~4-10 weeks, if the cycle reaches it |

Broad altcoin season and altseason mania are not the same thing.

Broad altcoin season means persistent participation spreading across large caps, midcaps and increasingly smaller assets. Capital is genuinely transmitting through the ecosystem rather than remaining concentrated in BTC and ETH.

Altseason mania comes later.

That is the phase most people remember: widespread speculation, rapidly multiplying narratives, accelerating microcaps and frequent +100-300% moves.

We are not there yet.

And broad altseason is not my base case for this week or next week.

The current working estimate places that transition roughly 2-6 weeks ahead, provided ETH/BTC remains structurally repaired, breadth survives repeated tests and capital progressively moves away from BTC concentration.

That makes the present phase particularly interesting.

The opportunity is not to wait until every altcoin is already vertical.

It is to identify the transition while capital is moving from ETH and large caps toward the next layers of the market.

## 🔬 WHAT THE FRAMEWORK IS LEARNING

Last week exposed both a strength and a weakness in the framework.

The sequence model understood the transition far better than the range model understood its magnitude.

Price acceleration was dramatically underestimated once the breakout started feeding on improving liquidity and momentum.

That is now a live calibration case.

At the same time, the week reinforced why I do not want to reduce altseason to a single indicator.

During the developing move, broad Top-100 participation moved roughly:

**80% → 46% → 6% → 42% → 73%**

ETH/BTC alone could not tell us that story.

The research layer is therefore testing whether ETH/BTC, breadth, BTC dominance, ETF flows and leverage each provide genuinely incremental information, or whether some of those layers merely add complexity.

Nothing gets promoted because it sounds sophisticated.

The market has to prove it prospectively.
