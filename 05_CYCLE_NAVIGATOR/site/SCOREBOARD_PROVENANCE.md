# Public scoreboard provenance

The public scoreboard is a presentation layer, `authority=false`.

Current seed rows CN15–CN20 are bound to contemporaneous publication records. CN16–CN20 original/recovered publications are stored under `05_CYCLE_NAVIGATOR/published/2026/`; CN21 canonically records the CN20 evaluation.

The public renderer intentionally shows only fields evidenced by the publication record. A dash means unavailable, not zero.

Historical policy:
- preserve published scores exactly as published;
- never recompute old publications under a later scoring contract;
- never combine non-comparable eras into one all-time precision percentage;
- do not infer missing forecasts from outcomes;
- open/not-matured claims do not enter completed denominators.

Future work may expand the displayed history only by adding provenance-qualified records from the historical census. Expansion must not weaken these rules.
