## Mandatory plain-language terminology
- **Officiel handling / Official action:** A valid, source-bound Compass output. Not a personal transaction instruction or auto-execution.
- **Aktuel retning / Current momentum:** Current observed directional classification, **not** a validated prediction of the next 12 hours.
- **Frossen prognose / Frozen forecast:** Original issued source values from before the relevant market outcomes. If no original daily points exist, **no** blue forecast line.
- **Observeret pris / Observed price:** Eligible source-verifiable hourly price closes with an as-of time; not a forecast, not a promise of real-time tick quotes.
- **Frosset prisinterval / Frozen range:** The original week/window high-low forecast band. A band is not the same as a point forecast.
- **Kurssfaldsrisiko / Pullback risk:** Ordinal source classification; not an event probability, expected loss amount or a SELL command.
- **Distribution risk:** Source's risk assessment of distribution conditions. Not automatically a confirmed exit.
- **SELL / TRIM:** Separate explicit source-owned assessment, never deduced from just pullback or distribution.
- **Price Range 70/30:** Existing method-specific score for price ranges; not trading P&L, market direction accuracy or statistical alpha.
- **PROVISIONAL / LIVE:** Week still developing. No final accuracy assertion.
- **DEGRADED:** Evidence has known coverage/source limitations; actionable confidence must be reduced or withheld according to upstream policy.
- **STALE:** The relevant underlying source observation/decision is outside its verified freshness policy, regardless of site/CDN build timestamp.
- **UNAVAILABLE / ABSTAIN:** Absent or ineligible evidence. Never treat as neutral market confirmation, safe HOLD or accurate outcome.
