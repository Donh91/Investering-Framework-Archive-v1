# Public product target

Within seconds a mobile visitor should be able to answer:

1. What should I do now?
2. What is expected over 12h, 1–3d and 5–7d?
3. Where should risk sit from Bitcoin through microcaps?
4. What needs to happen before the next rotation step?
5. When was this Compass issued, and when is the next meaningful reassessment window?

Everything else belongs in PATH or PROOF.
