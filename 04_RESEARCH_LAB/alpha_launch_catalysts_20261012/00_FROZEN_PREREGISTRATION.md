P5 **Public-project→CA binding requires publicly authenticated project-controlled wallet/claim.** Expect 0 authentic cases where brand/ticker similarity ALONE proves the future exact CA. Require reconstructible entry and sale route and observed timestamps; if unmeasurable mark UNKNOWN, not loss/win.

P7 **Causal attribution is asymmetric.** Tweets/KOL/announcements timestamped after price move are explanations/hype amplification, NOT proven launch catalysts. Timing must be source-availability before first response, and cross-source corroboration, else hypothesis only.

## Frozen causal taxonomy (allow multiple; no forced binary)
A = first-party product/utility and observed adoption/revenue *before price rise*;
B = community / organic social propagation evidenced across independent actors;
C = KOL, paid campaigns or influencer distribution (paid vs organic UNKNOWN unless verified);
D = coordination/supply concentration (mechanistic wallet cluster; may be bots/LP/deployer not common owners);
E = venue/liquidity mechanism (bonding curves, graduation, CEX/DEX listings, AMM depth, unlocks);
F = market-wide cycle/narrative/rotation;
G = unknown/unattributable, insufficient timestamped evidence.
"cabal" may be used only with an explicit **supported common-control** chain beyond co-occurring early buys, and even then avoid identifying real people or asserting criminal conduct.

1. Verify exact chain, original contract/mint, initial deploy/launch event and chain timestamps. Wrapped assets and ticker clones excluded.
2. Collect contemporaneous launch artifacts, dated first-party statements, early social timestamps (if historically demonstrable), earliest liquidity venue, milestones, holder/buyer provenance and sources available at each landmark.
3. Reconstruct 0–5m, 5–30m, 30m–24h, 1–7d, 7–30d where coverage supports it. Record nulls and censoring, not invented precision. Separately record market cap, FDV, peak, volume, token migration and fees when verifiable.
4. Validate public wallet links: funding sources, same-block share, developer direct buys, buyer uniqueness, repeated activity, coordinated swap patterns, concentration, sellability, observed independent market buys. Never equate 'new wallet' with independent owner; known CEX/bridge/router links are ambiguous.
5. For EACH case supply evidence tier E3 official onchain/docs / E2 corroborated contemporaneous / E1 secondary/anecdotal / E0 hypothesis, links and timestamps. Contradictory evidence and unknowns stay visible.
6. **Controls:** aim 1 matched loser per winning case on same chain, venue/factory and +/- 7d launch cohort, matched initial liquidity and trading eligibility. If no provider universe or date coverage, controls NOT FOUND and no causal/incremental prediction validation. No cherry-picked loser named after outcomes.
7. Never claim a price multiple is executable profit without fixed as-of decision timestamp, entry/exit route, actual liquidity/slippage/fees and no lookahead.
8. Base classifier cannot use ex-post 'winner' label as feature. Any learning-loop rule must explicitly contain prospective test, kill threshold, assumed cost/latency and population where it could generalize. Examples are not a BUY trigger.
9. Recheck alternative hypotheses (organic attention vs bot coordination; authentic issuer vs impersonator, bridge/LP transfer vs wash, age of project vs age of token, observed vs theoretical purchase).
