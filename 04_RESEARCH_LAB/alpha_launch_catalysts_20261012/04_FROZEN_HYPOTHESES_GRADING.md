## State vocabulary

- `DIRECTIONALLY_SUPPORTED_AS_MECHANISM`: source evidence demonstrates the proposed *mechanism in at least one case*; not causal counterfactual, population rate or tradable alpha.
- `CONTRADICTED_AS_GENERAL_SHORTCUT`: evidence rebuts a popular simplified assumption, but formal pre-frozen hypothesis may still require controls.
- `UNTESTABLE_WITH_THIS_SELECTION`: raw early cohort, source-available clocks, matched controls or fair execution absent.
- `OPEN_NOT_CONFIRMED`: hypothesis remains plausible but no adequate counterfactual.
- `FALSE`: a genuinely falsified specific prediction, with explicit data proving opposition (none can be claimed for most original P1–P7 given data omissions).

1. `DISCOVERY_MINTFOLIO_ECONOMIC_BRIDGE_GAP`: protocol fee 90/10 distribution to creator/treasury does not prove tokenholder revenue; app usage not enough.
2. `DISCOVERY_ASKR_FIRST_PURCHASE_EXCEPTION`: 99% early tax exception means onchain founder-first 5.46% launch purchase cannot be interpreted as independent buyer dominance. Public launch documents post-launch.
3. `DISCOVERY_FARTCOIN_CONCEPT_VS_CA`: pre-existing AI meme label weeks/months prior to unrelated anonymous later deployer; early project name discovery != issuer authentication.
4. `DISCOVERY_BONK_AIRDROP_BIAS`: 50% airdrop and 20% contributor allocation mean holder timestamps and wallet returns cannot be inferred as independent trader skill.
5. `DISCOVERY_PEPE_MULTISIG_RISK`: later multi-sig withdrawal/gov adjustment can harm even successful social token; not proof founders orchestrated initial winner.
6. `DISCOVERY_MOG_LATE_ALPHA_WINDOW`: major reported whale entry ~6mo after launch; CA-first advantage not a prerequisite to large 2024 repricing. However historic selection bias remains.
7. `DISCOVERY_TURBO_CROSS_CASE_LOSER`: one PEPE-winning trader reportedly lost ~136ETH on TURBO; skill must be measured across all trades including negatives.
8. `DISCOVERY_LATE_LISTING_NOT_INITIAL_CA`: dated Coinbase/Binance 2023/24 can create second peaks but not explain coin launch months earlier.

## Cabal/insider adjudication

**Firm positive findings (no criminal implication):** ASKR founder first-buy exception disclosed by issuer; BONK original contributor allocation; PEPE team Safe changed and moved tokens months after launch; MOG two Ethereum wallets had actual reciprocal transfers and economic link (from prior internal replay). None by itself satisfies “same beneficial owner behind launch, secretly controlled coordinated buyer swarm, with privileged information”.

**Firm negative findings:** `Cabal-proven cases = 0` **out of nine investigated with insufficient wallet coverage**. This is NOT “nine cases were organic”, NOT statistically meaningful zero cabal prevalence, and does not exonerate suspected schemes. Accurate state `CABAL_UNPROVEN_9_OF_9`, `FULL_WALLET_FORENSICS_0_OF_9`.

**Required next case-specific raw tests:** reconstruct initial buyer cohorts, funding graph with CEX exclusions, token/team wallets, repeated synchronized buy/sell patterns and realized exits, then apply supported/probable/unknown labels. Only call `CABAL_SUPPORTED` if common control backed by direct independent authenticated control evidence; coordination evidence alone warrants `CLUSTER_CANDIDATE` or `CLUSTER_SUPPORTED`, not identities or wrongdoing.
