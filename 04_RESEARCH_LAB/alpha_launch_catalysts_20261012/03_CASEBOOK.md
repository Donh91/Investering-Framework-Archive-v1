## R1 — ASKR: an issuer-first purchase masquerading as momentum

**What happened and what we can know:** First-party launch explanation says founder used Pons launch-and-buy, acquiring 5.46% in the first transaction, with a 99% anti-bot tax for 3 seconds from which the first team purchase was exempt. Distinct public chain identity 4663 and contract above; bond curve migrated after raising 4.2 ETH. Source: https://heyaskr.ai/docs/token/launch and https://heyaskr.ai/docs/token. A prior internal launch report says migration in ~98s and 11.4× theoretical FDV but those numbers are E1 carried forward, not independently reconstructed. Coingecko snapshots Sept19→25 moved only 1.07×.

**Freeze hypothesis check:** R1 E+D is directionally well supported for *mechanics*; it is NOT proof non-team demand was significant. Developer first buy is first-party disclosed, not proof fraud/cabal/hidden insiders. Core problem for Alpha Lab is actionability: a human or a 5-minute scanner cannot claim fair first-block entry under this fee design.

**What to learn:** `ISSUER_INITIAL_BUNDLE`, `FIRST_SECS_RESTRICTED_TRADING`, `GRADUATION_TIME`, `FIRST_INDEPENDENT_TRADE_AFTER_BOT_TAX`, `PUBLIC_TRADABLE_ENTRY_TIMECLOCK`; require quote/route and sells. **Specific falsifier:** timestamp signed factory and first 100 buy/sell logs with buyer funding independent from deployer; only count buys after launch as genuine market buyers.

## R2 — MINTFOLIO: good product ≠ good token without token value rights

**What happened:** Official app/docs show actual onchain basket creation/fees, verified protocol contracts (basket factory listed separately) and 90/10 creator/treasury split. Yet docs do not establish that the listed `MINTFOLIO` trading CA owns fee streams, redemption, treasury, governance, or a burn. Sources: https://docs.mintfolio.io/addresses, https://docs.mintfolio.io/learn/creator-earnings, https://docs.mintfolio.io/learn/fees, https://mintfolio.io/ . Current app counters are mutable and must not be smuggled into October2 launch.

**Freeze hypothesis check:** A (real product) is descriptively supported **at protocol level**, but cannot be admitted as token utility or source of purchase pressure. The pre-frozen R2 optimistic A+E value-bridge is **NOT SUPPORTED / DISCONFIRMATION OF IMPLIED TOKEN CAPTURE**, until first-party exact CA/token revenue/equity linkage and payment tx verified.

**Lesson:** Track `APP_VERIFIED`, `ISSUER_CA_AUTHENTICATED`, `TOKEN_REVENUE_CLAIM_VERIFIED` as three independent fields. A team may be genuine while a same-brand token is unrelated, overvalued or economically disconnected. No insider verdict.

## R3 — HARMONIC: recurring fee/burn narrative needs receipts

Official terms separate token ownership from revenue or enterprise equity. Website says autonomous agent claims creator fees, buys token and burns it. E3 evidence of **the claim**, NOT E3 evidence of verified execution/revenue. Verify the agent's owner wallet, fee recipient, swaps and burn events; exclude self-funded same-wallet churn and classify whether marginal fee users are independent. https://harmonicagent.solutions/ , https://harmonicagent.solutions/terms.html . Historical independent snapshots ~1.78× Sept14→Oct10; the unhosted launch seed multiple is only E1 theoretical.

**Lesson:** `PROTOCOL_REAL_VALUE_FLOW` requires **actual** externally sourced fees, verifiable swap route and canonical burn. Claimed burn ≠ proven organic buyer, and even real burns may be smaller than insider distribution.

## S1 — BONK: fair distribution ≠ fair initial buyer cohort

BONK's original source allocation committed 50% to selected Solana community airdrop groups while allocating 20% to early contributors with 3-year vesting. That is a **community distribution decision**, not ordinary public market buying. Launch after FTX failure was a reputational/narrative catalyst; a later large December2023 run coincided with Binance/Coinbase/Saga narratives. Coinbase Dec14 2023 reporting follows 2022 launch by nearly a year. https://bonkcoin.com/BONK-Paper.pdf, https://streamflow.finance/blog/bonk-token-vesting, https://www.coindesk.com/markets/2023/12/14/meme-coin-bonk-spikes-40-on-coinbase-listing .

**Lesson:** Strong social/community catalysts may be durable even without a cashflow product. But categorize `DISTRIBUTION_AIRDROP`, `EARLY_CONTRIBUTOR_ALLOCATION`, `UNLOCK_VESTING` before classifying wallets as early smart-money. An airdrop recipient's cheap coins are not a 100× trading skill observation.

## E1 — PEPE: enormous social winner and later project-wallet risk

PEPE original Ethereum contract and ~93.1% LP vs 6.9% team reserve reported token allocation. Fast viral diffusion generated repricing already underway before early-May Binance spot. Later Aug24 2023 team-controlled Safe transfers roughly 16T PEPE to exchange-linked wallets and changed signer threshold; contemporaneous public reporting linked this to sharp drop. This establishes real concentration/governance risk **after launch** but does not establish an initial coordinated illegal “cabal”. https://etherscan.io/token/0x6982508145454ce325ddbe47a25d4ec3d2311933 , https://www.binance.com/en/square/post/503066 , https://cointelegraph.com/news/pepe-token-plunges-strange-token-transfers-fears-rug-pull .

**Lesson:** `PROJECT_SAFE_WITHDRAWALS`, `SIGNER_THRESHOLD_CHANGE`, `PROJECT_UNEXPECTED_CEX_DEPOSIT` are valuable distinct negative risk features even for winners. Historical May6 $3.81e-6 vs Aug26 $0.88e-6 shows approx **-77%** from chosen later price high; an early winner may create long subsequent large drawdowns. Allegations of specific insiders stealing treasury remain unadjudicated.

## E3 — TURBO: strong narrative and a famous buyer who still lost

TURBO's $69 GPT-4 frog creation was unusually memorable distribution content; real token had no AI revenue technology simply because the meme had AI creative provenance. May2023 press documents subsequent community fundraising and >$30m market cap. Prior Lookonchain-style analyst reported a successful PEPE trader `qianbaidu.eth` purchased 382 ETH TURBO and later sold for 246 ETH, losing **~136 ETH** in this *single trade* — independently replay needed, but direction crucial. https://turbotoken.io/ , https://cryptoslate.com/chatgpt-created-turbo-memecoin-crosses-50m-marketcap/ , https://threadreaderapp.com/thread/1655424794559668224.html .

Token itself ultimately appreciated ~14.79× between **May05 2023 and Sept17 2024** snapshots, proving a loser *trade* can coexist with a winning *token*, and vice versa.

## Cross-chain structural insights (not yet validated by matched controls)
1. **Information window ≠ execution window:** ASKR has short-lived anti-bot tax while fast chain creates CA in same founder-bundle; Ethereum tokens can have high MEV/gas/LP slippage; Solana pools/mints require creator, priority-fee and bundle scrutiny. In all cases launch scanner success alone is not profit.
2. **Two-wave causality:** BONK and WIF have well-dated *later* CEX amplification, MOG slow-repricing and TURBO meme persistence. “Who launched first” may be less economically relevant than “who recognized durable second wave early” — a falsifiable research direction, not a proven edge.
3. **Real project ≠ tokenholder economics:** MINTFOLIO and HARMONIC are necessary stress tests for “good team” selection: exact token value capture/cashflow cannot be inferred from product marketing.
4. **Coordination ≠ guilt:** BONK allocations, ASKR first party initial bundles, PEPE Safe controls and MOG linked wallet trades have very different mechanisms. Research must separate **source-of-tokens**, **economic link**, **common owner**, **issuer relationship**, **buy/sell cashflow**, **information timing** and **unlawful privilege**.
5. **Historical winners cannot train predictive weights alone:** Need matched same-chain/source-window losing launches, with original as-of feature cutoffs, prospectively frozen before price followup. Until then causal and skill claims remain NOT TESTED.
