## Most actionable—not yet live alpha—observations
- **ASKR:** issuer-disclosed bundled first purchase exempted from first-3s 99% tax means first buys cannot be called ordinary accessible demand.
- **MINTFOLIO:** protocol works and creators receive fee splits; listed token's claim on fees/revenue not documented, so app utility != investible token cashflow.
- **HARMONIC:** claimed buy-and-burn/fee revenue needs direct onchain swap and burn receipts, not marketing words or seed-price multiples.
- **BONK:** airdrop and early contributor vesting mean wallet 'early' status is often allocation, not alpha.
- **WIF:** dated Binance listing was a *later* amplification, not genesis cause. Independent holder growth still needs raw wallet review.
- **FARTCOIN:** meme idea predated token by months; no authenticated project-root→specific deployer/CA connection; anonymous issuer 'no cabal' claim unresolved.
- **PEPE:** late team multisig transfer/governance change undermined token safety after successful initial pump; no proof it was initial cabal.
- **MOG:** large gains materialized months after original launch; reportedly successful smart wallets entered months later, countering CA-first-only selection.
- **TURBO:** notable early narrative winner but a famous prior PEPE winning wallet reportedly lost ~136 ETH trading TURBO; a wallet's *one* win != trade skill.

## Acceptance boundaries
- Nine outcome-selected examples cannot prove predictor edge, frequencies or causal drivers; missing scientifically matched losers.
- No earliest verified full liquidity/independent wallet graph in nine cases, no realized exit simulation; never assign `INSIDER/CABAL_PROVEN`, `VERIFIED_BUY`, `TRADEABLE_10X` from this casebook.
- Better hypothesis: timing and authenticity of **independent sustained attention/buys** and **later repricing waves** on a chain-specific, objectively sampled cohort; test rather than ship.
