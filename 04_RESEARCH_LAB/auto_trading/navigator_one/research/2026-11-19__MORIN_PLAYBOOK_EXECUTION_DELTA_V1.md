## 4. Three candidate primitives, not seven competing engines
A. RANGE_REJECTION / RECLAIM (primary candidate, #228/#98 and Navigator #937). Define causal state machine IDLE -> LEVEL_FROZEN -> TEST -> SWEEP/NO_SWEEP -> RECLAIM -> FLOW_CONFIRM or ABSTAIN -> hypothetical entry eligibility -> invalidation/timeout. L=previous confirmed low, frozen only after right-hand pivot confirmation; level age and volatility observed before test. ATR or robust trailing log-return scale known before current bar; no future swing selection. Sweep if new trade/low < L - epsilon, then closes/reclaims > L + buffer within tau; no-sweep branch within epsilon from above and separate higher-low/rejection criterion. epsilon/buffer based on tick size/volatility, no tuning heldout. Define tie and expiry once, one event per frozen level/rearm rule. Nested range Mouse Trap = same primitive with frozen local boundary inside an already-known POI; mirror high-side rejection as exit-risk or paper short in eligible instrument only (no microcap short assumed). For three drives: label three previously confirmed swing excursions with diminishing displacement or flow-adjusted reclaim; secondary challenger only, no fourth complex detector. Rounded retest: zone frozen from past price/volume, quantifiable deceleration plus retest/rejection and invalidation; secondary variant, don't fit curves by eye. Divergence RSI/AO derived from price, correlated, not two independent votes; price-only ablation first.
B. FLOW_EFFICIENCY / ABSORPTION CHALLENGER (shared concept, venue-specific computation). For each complete time or event-count bin: signed quote notional D=sum(s_i*p_i*q_i), V=sum(p_i*q_i), d=D/V. s=+1 taker buy,-1 taker sell. DEX use actual signed quote inflow/outflow from eligible swap routes; exclude nontrades (transfers/LP/curve completion), suppress double-counted routed legs, separate uncertain related/self-funded flows as risk, do not follow coordinated actors. Bin by price level/time with fixed tick grid. CEX L2 optional OFI (best-bid/ask add/cancel/trade events) plus depth/replenishment; DEX liquidity and executable quote impact; separate semantics. Research feature sign-adjusted return residual from train-only robust baseline impact versus signed flow, volume, spread/depth/size, instrument/age/regime. For candidate sell absorption: strong negative delta with small price deterioration relative to expected impact, followed by confirmed reclaim; inverse at top. Robust rolling ranks of D and V conditioned on venue, age and past observations; no division by near-zero flow, V=0 or unknown -> abstain. Heavy negative flow without sustained recovery or liquidity -> veto, not accumulation. Distinguish net flow from independent retained demand; independent participation/retained capital only if source and privacy admitted. Missing L2 => trade-only proxy label, NOT absorption truth or synthetic footprint fill.
