**Interpretation:** Solid arrows show existing conceptual data/evidence ownership, not proof of a current production run or data sharing permission. Dotted arrows are proposed research relationships subject to current gates. The restricted repository never sends raw private values to the public control plane. The prototype has no direct connection to private data or live Alpha decision paths.
