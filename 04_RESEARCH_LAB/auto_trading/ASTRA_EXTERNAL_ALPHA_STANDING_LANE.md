## Mission

Treat external successful traders, bots, wallet cohorts and strategy repositories as research objects that may contain transferable edge primitives.

Do not begin from the premise that a successful external system should be copied.

Begin from:

`Can its observed behavior be reconstructed point-in-time, can the claimed edge survive simple controls, and can any small mechanism add independent value to our Framework?`

## Discovery responsibility

Astra may discover new external candidates from public sources when the active research queue has capacity.

For every new candidate:

1. deduplicate against the registry and source notes;
2. produce an intake packet matching `external_alpha/CANDIDATE_INTAKE_SCHEMA.json`;
3. independently classify evidence quality;
4. update or propose registry evidence;
5. run the deterministic admission scorer;
6. only spend heavy modeling budget if the candidate earns it.

Default prior remains reject/watch.

## Deep-research allocation

Use heavy reasoning where it changes adjudication:

- ledger reconstruction;
- timestamp/leakage forensics;
- opportunity-set reconstruction;
- causal/mechanistic decomposition;
- source-vs-copy execution analysis;
- version/era separation;
- cross-case primitive attribution.

Do not burn heavy-model budget on:

- paraphrasing marketing posts;
- generic architecture diagrams;
- speculative hidden-feature stories;
- duplicated repo surveys;
- fitting large models before clean controls exist.

## Cross-case learning

Do not start a strategy-genome synthesis until the machine-readable meta-learning gate is `UNLOCKED`.

When unlocked, analyze primitives rather than brands and preserve dependency information between cases.

Repeated use of the same upstream feed, copied codebase or wallet cohort is not independent corroboration.

## Expected long-run output

A growing library of:

- verified source-edge cases;
- rejected/failed cases;
- copyability and capacity rulings;
- reusable edge primitives;
- primitive-by-regime scorecards;
- transfer experiments against existing Framework owners;
- a gated cross-strategy empirical strategy genome.

The end goal is not an ensemble of copied bots.

The end goal is a Framework-native AI trading mechanism whose components have survived independent falsification across our own evidence and, where useful, multiple external proven systems.
