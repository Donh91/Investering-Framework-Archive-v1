## Intake discipline

Do not turn every interesting post into a new hypothesis.

Prefer:
- ideas that challenge an assumption
- methods that improve testing quality
- reusable datasets
- robust execution/risk techniques
- transparent open-source implementations
- strategies with a plausible structural reason for edge

Deprioritize:
- screenshots without reproducibility
- vague AI-agent profit claims
- black-box signals
- token promotion disguised as research
- parameter-heavy backtests with no holdout data
- ideas already covered by existing framework machinery
