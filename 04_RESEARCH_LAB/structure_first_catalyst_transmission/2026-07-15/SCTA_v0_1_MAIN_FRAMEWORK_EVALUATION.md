The audit is valuable, but its strongest valid conclusion is not that the S0-S3 taxonomy works as a decision engine. The strongest conclusion is that it remains a plausible mechanism hypothesis while having produced no demonstrated incremental decision value beyond the existing completed-close and persistence gates.

## Accepted findings

1. **The Type-2 mechanism remains plausible.** In the curated sample, the cases classified as structure repaired before the catalyst show materially better follow-through than weak-structure normal-catalyst cases.
2. **Regime-scale catalysts are a real boundary condition.** Liquidity backstops and policy reversals can dominate pre-existing structure and must remain out of scope for the Type-2 claim.
3. **S3 requires a pre-frozen maturity split.** Early expansion and late-cycle euphoria behave differently, but this split was discovered retrospectively and cannot be used prospectively until assignment rules are frozen.
4. **Seven days is insufficient for durability evaluation in this dataset.** Two 2026 failures looked durable at 7D and fully retraced on days 8-13. The July-14 holdout should therefore be evaluated at both 7D and 12 completed sessions.
5. **The redundancy finding is the highest-value result.** Every documented live save cited by the audit was also generated by the existing close-basis and persistence gates. OTA currently improves explanation and falsification, not execution decisions.

## Methodological downgrades

### Headline sample size overstates evidentiary strength

The CSV contains 32 historical rows, but:

- 25 rows are Tier B with source value `TRAINING_DOCUMENTED`;
- those rows have approximate returns and no event-level primary-source register;
- historical breadth, funding and open interest are missing;
- only one dataset row, A07, explicitly includes `LIVE_LOG` in the source field;
- the July-14 holdout is the second clean pre-registered prospective case.

Therefore the historical sample is useful for hypothesis generation and failure-atlas construction, but not sufficient for causal or rule-promotion claims.

### Internal classification inconsistency

The report summarizes `S0 + hot catalyst` as 2/2 catastrophic using B05 and A04, with B04/B08 called adjacent. However A04 is classified `S1_BTC_ONLY` in the dataset, while B04 is explicitly classified `S0`. The headline row should therefore be treated as an informal cluster, not a clean S0 conditional statistic.

### Prospective-source inconsistency

The report states A06 and A07 were pre-registered in real time. The dataset source field marks only A07 as `FMP_OHLC+FARSIDE+LIVE_LOG`; A06 is `FMP_OHLC+FARSIDE`. A06 must not be counted as prospectively registered unless a readable pre-event receipt is linked separately.

### Outcome taxonomy is too flexible

The 32 rows use 23 distinct `outcome_class` labels. This is descriptive but weakens reproducibility and makes post-hoc category selection too easy. Before the next prospective event, evaluation should use a frozen compact outcome set, for example:

```text
DURABLE_EXPANSION
NARROW_OR_BTC_ONLY_DURABLE
SQUEEZE_OR_PARTIAL_RETRACE
FULL_RETRACE
NEGATIVE_FOLLOW_THROUGH
REGIME_CATALYST_OUT_OF_SCOPE
```

This is an evaluation schema only and must not become a new market engine.

### Matched-case analysis is qualitative

The report provides useful paired examples, but no fixed matching distance, surprise normalization or formal control for volatility and starting regime. `Correlationally strong` should therefore be read as `suggestive within a curated sample`, not as a statistical estimate.

### Decision-value claims require canonical receipts

The 4/4 live-save narrative is potentially important, but it is not reproducible from the SCTA CSV alone. Any future decision-value table must link each save to:

- pre-event framework receipt;
- frozen gate state;
- decision that was actually available at the time;
- mature outcome timestamp;
- counterfactual decision cost.

## Frozen July-14 holdout

**Research classification:** `S2 / BORDERLINE_S3_INTRADAY_UNCONFIRMED`  
**Authority:** shadow only

### P1 — structure hypothesis

If completed BTC and ETH/BTC closes confirm within 48 hours and BTC ETF flow stops contradicting within two completed sessions, expect at least 50% move retention at 7D, no full retrace below approximately 62.2K, and durability through 12 completed sessions.

### P2 — flow contradiction override

If BTC ETF flows remain negative across the next two completed sessions, expect an extended pop followed by a full retrace to approximately 62.2K or lower within 8-12 sessions.

### P3 — flow-necessity falsifier

If flows continue to contradict but the move remains durable through 12 sessions, the flow leg is unnecessary for this mechanism and the model should be simplified.

### Maturity marks

```text
24H: approximately 2026-07-15 evening
72H: approximately 2026-07-17
7D: approximately 2026-07-21
12 completed sessions: approximately 2026-07-30
```

The July 28-29 FOMC sits inside the 12-session window and must be logged as a potential attribution confound rather than silently absorbed.

## Kill and merge criteria

1. If two of the first three pre-registered prospective S2/S3 cases fail their frozen durability predictions at 12 sessions, retire the `LEADING` claim.
2. If the class cannot be assigned unambiguously before two consecutive events from truth-layer fields alone, retire the taxonomy as non-reproducible.
3. If the next five catalyst events yield decisions identical to the existing completed-close and persistence gates, merge the language into gate documentation and stop maintaining OTA as a separate taxonomy.
4. Regime-scale policy or liquidity reversals remain permanently out of scope.

## Operational decision

- Do not create a new engine, threshold, sensor, weight or score.
- Do not run another broad historical audit now.
- Evaluate the frozen July-14 case only at its maturity marks.
- Require one additional pre-registered prospective case before the n=3 re-audit.
- Preserve OTA as a causal-hypothesis, failure-atlas and adversarial-audit layer only.
- The taxonomy earns permanence only if it changes a decision that existing gates would otherwise get wrong.
