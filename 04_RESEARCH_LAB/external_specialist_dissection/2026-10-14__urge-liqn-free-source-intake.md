The public method describes five wallet-activity changes versus prior time window: number of distinct buying wallets, number of buy trades, net buy/sell flow, returning buyer interest, fewer sellers. ## 2. LIQN hypothesis, cheap first

Niche competence worth investigating: *factor-level risk dispersion, crowding and sequential market-stress deterioration*, not LIQN's private UI or composite score.

**Preliminary null hypothesis:** A simple free, reproducible U.S. high-beta equity risk proxy does **not** improve crypto pullback alert precision, warning lead time, or false-positive rate after accounting for existing M6/Spot-Perp/Pullback inputs.

**Candidate free observable proxies, subject to existing owner/source review:** daily broad-index vs high-beta/small-cap relative returns, equal-risk breadth/drawdown and realized dispersion. These are hypotheses only. Use **existing approved source timestamps and snapshots**, not scraped proprietary LIQN endpoints. If the repository does not already have admissible point-in-time coverage, state `INPUT_SOURCE_MISSING`, do not backfill a purported live signal.

**Experiment path, under S5/M6 owner:**
1. Freeze exact source availability time for each factor proxy and for each existing crypto comparator at decision cutoff. Account for equity session timing, holidays, revisions and publication lag.
2. Choose a fixed negative-control comparison and market-state-stratified denominator, including choppy non-pullbacks, actual stress, and false risk-on days; no winner-only event collection.
3. Baseline must be the **existing** M6/pullback policy, plus a simple equity volatility/breadth control. Evaluate extra information conditional on BTC/ETH crypto-internal stress, ETF flows, spot/perp and regime.
4. Outcomes at frozen +24h/+72h/+7d windows where authorized; compare precision/recall, alert lead time, false alarm rate, calibrated probabilities and missed actionable downside. Apply fee/slippage/decision consequences only where defensible and separately labelled.
5. Use temporal holdout with purging of overlapping horizons and unchanged thresholds. **No training on later revised LIQN values**.
6. Reject if data are unavailable, effect is unstable or candidate adds no incremental forward value. Retain methodological framing only if useful.

**Gate:** `LIQN_ACCESS_STATE=LOGIN_REQUIRED`; `FREE_OFFICIAL_API=UNVERIFIED`; `PROSPECTIVE_PERFORMANCE=NOT_TESTED`. Nothing goes into live Kompas/Cycle Navigator/Pullback until independent evidence, review and owner sign-off.
