## Executive design: an Alpha Lab truth advantage

Our target is not a platform that looks like Nansen + Bubblemaps + GMGN + Axiom. Our target is **demonstrably better prospective decisions with a complete denominator and realistic executable outcomes**.

Four question layers:
1. **Discoverability:** could the system have *actually known* this exact CA, issuer, canonical pool and market at time t, before the pump or promotion? Track missed winners, skipped/unread/never-launched cases, source latency.
2. **Launch truth:** what exactly happened at origin? Freeze chain-first launch funding, same-tx atomic events, first-block order, owner/deployer balances and dispenser paths; do **not** retroactively erase history when wallets exit.
3. **Current exit truth:** who can still distribute, where did supply move, what executable buy/sell paths and size capacity existed *at cutoff*, and what does independent simulation or raw trace prove? "Provider SAFE" and liquid-looking UI are not enough.
4. **Realized edge:** did each additive feature improve false-positive rejection and top-K useful discovery against its existing baseline without materially increasing missed winners, delayed qualification, API cost or unverifiable inference? Freeze predictions before outcomes.

**Two independent dimensions, never a single CLEAN:** `ORIGIN_RISK_IMMUTABLE` (historical first-observed launch/funding/ownership structure, source-versioned and timestamped) and `CURRENT_EXIT_RISK_DYNAMIC` (latest ownership/exits/liquidity/taxes/contracts/freshness). Historical evidence remains unchanged; a smaller current overhang does not make an engineered launch organic. Both may be UNKNOWN. This is a *method proposal* for existing risk/evidence owners, NOT a newly deployed field or score.

## REA: specific falsifiable hypotheses to deliver to current owners

**H1 / origin-risk persistence — S3 + S4 + MAL #88/#126.**  
For matched newly detected coins, compare no origin flag vs `ORIGIN_RISK_IMMUTABLE` derived from source-backed first-block ownership/funder/coordinated-distribution evidence. Separately observe `CURRENT_EXIT_RISK_DYNAMIC` at t. **Negative control:** an organic, independently funded early crowd that shares router/bridge infrastructure; don't conflate synchronized buys with common ownership. **Target:** earlier false-safe detection before -60–80% drawdowns with low false-rejection of organic future winners. **Stop** if it adds nothing above raw top-holder + liquidity + first-block concentration or is mostly UNKNOWN.

**H2 / first-buyer independence and survival — S2 + S4.**  
Use first-10/20/70 as **different denominator definitions** (do not combine); compare entity-adjusted *economically independent buyers* and their point-in-time retention/partial exits to naive early wallet count. Model first-sale latency and effective tax/cost, only if wallet/transfer history complete. **Negative control:** distinct buys from unrelated wallets through common aggregator; distributor-funded accounts not automatically common-control. **Target:** better qualification or less overhang-risk misses at +1h, +24h, +72h; terminate if latency or missingness erase gain.

**H3 / source disagreement and unread — X0 + Archive #1087.**  
Pair independent public source signals and internal chain-native facts by exact chain+CA+pool, with observed_at, available_at and source coverage. Classify `MATCH`, `PROVIDER_OVERCONFIDENT`, `INTERNAL_UNCERTAIN`, `IDENTITY_CONFLICT`, `STALE`, `UNKNOWN`. **Examples:** OPA CLEAN versus unresolved same-slot warning; OFY OK with 20 unread; aggregator liquidity omitted vs zero. **Stop** if mismatch simply tracks noisy sources and adds no graded result. **Never** call provider consensus independent chain truth.

**H4 / executable exit vs implied risk — S1.**  
For a fixed notional and *exact execution pool* at cutoff, require buy/sell route or credible explicit negative proof, dynamic tax/transfer behavior, slippage/gas, full simulator coverage and available_at. Match GoPlus/Honeypot to EVM native traces where supported. **Negative control:** trade inactivity alone, unsupported pair, incorrect quote, simulator error or unknown endpoint. **Target:** lower false SAFE and false NO_ROUTE with a non-zero eligible population. **Blocked by existing S1 independent negative proof / available prospective pair count**; no speculative “all safe” from one test.

**H5 / KOL and social attention conversion — S7 + S6.**  
Exact first social source publication (not discovery crawl time), verified official CA and contamination type; measure independent qualified inflow BEFORE versus AFTER promotions and verified early-wallet net exit. **Negative control:** same ticker/different CA (ASKR vs BASKR; GROKLET A vs B); bot-driven volume and purchased boosts. **Target:** improved identification of buy-before-post-distribute without blocking independently qualified projects. **Stop** if first social timestamp unrecoverable.

**H6 / owner-vs-address distribution illusion — S3 + S6.**  
For owner distribution to multiple wallets, compare address-holder-count growth vs economic-controller-adjusted concentration and later actual exit path. Known BOOMER control case in MAL #126: owner transferred majority into owned distribution contract and dispersed to many addresses; raw holder increase must not be independent adoption. **Negative controls:** disclosed airdrops, vesting, legitimate third-party programs. **Target:** identify misleading apparent adoption without alleging all recipients are Sybil or proven same owner.

**H7 / mutable exit state / code lineage — S1 + Archive #1087.**  
For auditable EVM contracts, compare source/proxy implementation/owner authority and meaningful state changes at launch, before quote and after early holder migration. Slither/Echidna/Phalcon are *method references*, not guarantees. **Negative control:** ordinary widely reused clone/factory and immutably controlled verified proxy. **Target:** uncover unsafe tax/blacklist/permission change paths *before* executable sellability fails. **Stop** if compiler/license/ABI/access prevents reproducible evidence.
