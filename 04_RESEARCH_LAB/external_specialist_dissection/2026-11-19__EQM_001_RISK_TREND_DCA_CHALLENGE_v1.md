Formula: x_t=ln(days since 2009-01-03); y_t=ln(close_t). Fit ordinary least squares of y on x using all rows strictly before t (min 730). Save e_t=y_t-fitted_y_t. Check 2026-11-17 rank 4.11%; 730d rolling alternative 76.16%; 1460d rolling 22.19%. All have different specification semantics. EMA span n, alpha=2/(n+1), seed first valid observation; no future data. EMA20/80 on price and R separately. At next daily open after signal t, target BTC weight:
- hold=1; priceTrend=1 if price EMA20 > EMA80 else 0; riskTrend=1 if R EMA20 > EMA80 else 0; riskWeight=1-R; combined=1 if both trend conditions true else 0; half=0.5. No leverage/short/interest. RiskWeight is daily rebalancing, NOT DCA. Positive risk momentum may reflect trend, not loss probability.
- Start wealth 1 in cash; each day turnover d=abs(target-prior weight after drift); wealth*=1-fee*d; next open mark r=O_{i+1}/O_i (last day mark close/open); wealth*=1+target*(r-1); drift weight=target*r/(1+target*(r-1)). Approximate proportional fee accounting, no taxes/price impact/spread beyond cost assumption, no exit charge, daily marks not intraday. First allocation fee included. ## Executed diagnostic B: contribution-matched accumulation

Spend min(cash,100*multiplier); remaining cash held without interest; buy BTC units=spend*(1-0.001)/open. Multipliers: fixed 1; lowRisk 2*(1-R); priceTrend and riskTrend respectively 1.5 if their trend condition holds else .5; combined 1.5 if both else .5.
