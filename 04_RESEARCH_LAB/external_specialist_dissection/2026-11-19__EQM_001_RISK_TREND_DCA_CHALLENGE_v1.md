- hold=1; priceTrend=1 if price EMA20 > EMA80 else 0; riskTrend=1 if R EMA20 > EMA80 else 0; riskWeight=1-R; combined=1 if both trend conditions true else 0; half=0.5. No leverage/short/interest. RiskWeight is daily rebalancing, NOT DCA. Positive risk momentum may reflect trend, not loss probability.

Spend min(cash,100*multiplier); remaining cash held without interest; buy BTC units=spend*(1-0.001)/open. Multipliers: fixed 1; lowRisk 2*(1-R); priceTrend and riskTrend respectively 1.5 if their trend condition holds else .5; combined 1.5 if both else .5.
