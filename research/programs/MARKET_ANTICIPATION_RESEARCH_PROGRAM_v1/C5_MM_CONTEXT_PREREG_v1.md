## Hypotheses and design
H0 = no C5: existing same-clock Official Compass downside-risk flag (HIGH/ELEVATED vs other explicitly classified states) unchanged. H1 research-only = H0 OR (VIX term structure INVERTED AND asset-specific observed long/short liquidation pressure HIGH for either window). Pressure HIGH >=2x, LOW <=0.5x, otherwise NORMAL relative to trailing median of disjoint 28 daily or 4 weekly windows; require available positive baseline and coverage evidence. If any required component or baseline is unavailable, exclude the matched pair; never infer a negative signal. Context does not amend H0 or the original forecast. Candidate threshold is fixed before enrollment.

Endpoint: adverse price move, defined as minimum observed UTC daily close at least 5% below original same-clock asset reference over days 1–7, 1–14, 1–21 and 1–28. One week primary, 2–3 weeks and 4 weeks secondary. Same venue and immutable close evidence for both arms; no incomplete outcome windows. BTC and ETH are paired observations within a weekly cluster, not independent weeks.

Holdout: enroll exactly the first eight eligible prospective weekly issue clusters within the first ten scheduled weekly slots after freeze. Eligibility is decided and recorded at the original issue cutoff using input provenance and complete paired BTC/ETH predictor availability only, without reference to any subsequent outcomes. Missing, ineligible or unpublished slots count in coverage; do not extend beyond ten scheduled slots. Stop enrollment at the eighth eligible cluster or the tenth scheduled slot, whichever occurs first. Coverage denominator is all scheduled slots from the first slot through that stopping slot. Freeze enrolled issue IDs at enrollment; no replacement for missing outcomes, no outcome-dependent extension or early evaluation. Evaluate once after 28-day maturity of the last enrollment (or last scheduled slot if fewer than eight enrolled); minimum N=8 distinct weekly clusters with mature paired outcomes per endpoint. If fewer than eight enrolled or any enrolled paired outcome is missing, report HOLD with reason and coverage, not a new cohort. No training or tuning. Primary statistic paired difference in mean binary Brier loss (0/1 flags); report per-asset and cluster means, sensitivity, specificity and false-positive delta with uncertainty; no claim of statistical significance from eight weeks. Secondary results cannot rescue primary failure. No threshold tuning on holdout. Any new version requires new preregistration and new prospective cohort.
