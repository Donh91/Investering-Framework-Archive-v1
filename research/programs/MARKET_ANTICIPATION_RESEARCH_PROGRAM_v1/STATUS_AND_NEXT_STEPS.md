# Status and Next Steps

## What is in place

1. Program identity, scope and non-replacement rule.
2. Seven complementary research tracks.
3. Ten ordered agent work packages.
4. Frozen research question, targets, horizons and failure conditions.
5. Point-in-time, publication-lag, vintage, purging, embargo and sealed-holdout requirements.
6. Incremental-value requirement against the current framework baseline.
7. Independent replication requirement.
8. Promotion states and zero-authority boundary before governance approval.
9. Explicit outputs for Master Monday and RAW forecast integration.
10. Archive location and branch for implementation.

## What remains before a final empirical conclusion

### Gate A — Inventory
- map exact existing datasets, sensors, ledgers and reusable code;
- identify overlaps with Global Liquidity, Cycle Navigator, Shadow Sentinel and DATA PING;
- bind each work package to existing owners where possible.

### Gate B — Data
- recover point-in-time ETF, CME, options, stablecoin, exchange, breadth, DeFi, macro and historical event data;
- preserve source receipts, hashes and knowledge timestamps;
- construct immutable owner tables and challenger tables.

### Gate C — Labels
- freeze event definitions before inspecting outcomes;
- create failed-move, stress and transition event ledgers;
- audit label ambiguity and class imbalance.

### Gate D — Methods
- implement reproducible feature builders;
- run synthetic controls first;
- add leakage, multiplicity, specification and leave-one-era-out tests.

### Gate E — Economic execution
- execute the frozen contract on development windows;
- keep final holdout sealed;
- compare against the framework baseline and simple naive baselines.

### Gate F — Replication
- blind second-agent replication;
- discrepancy adjudication;
- immutable release package.

### Gate G — Final archive report
- conclusion per research track;
- cross-track synthesis;
- rejected ideas and null results;
- recommended prospective shadow candidates;
- remaining research queue.

## Natural next research wave after completion

1. Cross-asset liquidity routing beyond crypto: Nasdaq, high yield, gold, dollar and rates.
2. Market microstructure transition: order-book resilience, liquidation topology and venue fragmentation.
3. Narrative-to-flow lag: whether social and research narratives lead actual capital routing or merely follow price.
4. Adaptive sensor weighting by regime uncertainty, with strict governance and no online self-modification.
5. Forecast disagreement engine: use disagreement among models as an uncertainty and transition signal.
6. Counterfactual policy simulator for exit/rebuy timing under the user's asymmetric drawdown preference.

## Current conclusion

The research program is architecturally ready but not empirically complete. No claim of predictive improvement is authorised until the data, leakage, walk-forward, holdout and replication gates are passed.
