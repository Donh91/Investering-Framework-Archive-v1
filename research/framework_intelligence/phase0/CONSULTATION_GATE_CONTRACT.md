# Master Monday Consultation Gate - Phase 0 Contract

The gate is advisory only.

Inputs must be point-in-time eligible and provenance-valid for the simulated week.

Allowed outputs:

- NO_MATERIAL_LEARNING
- WEEKLY_ANALYSIS_CONTEXT
- CONTRADICTION_CONTEXT
- RANGE_CONTEXT
- METHOD_IMPROVEMENT_CANDIDATE
- EXPERIMENT_ONLY
- NOT_EVALUABLE

The gate may not:

- change canonical phase/state;
- alter thresholds or model weights;
- reconstruct historical forecasts;
- turn descriptive historical evidence into prospective skill;
- count specialist commentary as independent evidence;
- hide uncertainty or source-quality limits.

A method-improvement finding is a future proposal only and has no effect on the simulated historical decision methodology.
