# Point-in-Time Firewall

For each simulated week, evidence is eligible only if its original durable timestamp is at or before that week's historical Master Monday decision cutoff.

Later outcomes may be attached only after the simulated decision is frozen and only in a clearly separated outcome-audit section.

A later generated summary, registry snapshot, learning state or adjudication file may not be used to infer what the framework knew earlier unless the underlying earlier immutable evidence is independently resolved and timestamp-valid.

AI commentary is interpretation, not an independent evidence event. Correlated metrics and semantic duplicate candidates must not inflate confirmation counts.

Any ambiguity about whether evidence existed at cutoff resolves to `NOT_EVALUABLE`, never reconstruction by assumption.
