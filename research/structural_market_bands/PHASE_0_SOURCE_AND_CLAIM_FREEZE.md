# STRUCTURAL MARKET BANDS - PHASE 0 SOURCE AND CLAIM FREEZE

**Status:** Completed  
**Date:** 2026-07-29  
**Parent specification:** `SMB_FUNCTIONAL_REPLICATION_v0_1.md`

## Purpose

Freeze what is publicly claimed, what can reasonably be tested, and what remains unknown before any model construction begins.

This prevents retrospective invention, proprietary-formula guessing and accidental overstatement.

## Claims accepted for research framing

The public description supports testing the following broad proposition:

- the model is based on on-chain data
- the model is intended primarily for UTXO-type blockchains
- coin lifespan or age structure is relevant
- short-, medium- and long-horizon market-cap behavior is relevant
- the output consists of dynamic structural support and resistance zones
- zones are described as structural potential or counter-pressure, not calibrated probabilities

These are research claims, not validated facts about predictive value.

## Unknowns frozen as unknown

The research must not assume knowledge of:

- exact formula
- exact input series
- exact cohort definitions
- exact weighting scheme
- exact time windows
- smoothing method
- normalization
- band width calculation
- band update cadence
- reliability calculation
- treatment of lost coins
- treatment of exchange/custodian UTXOs
- entity adjustment
- private provider transformations

## Prohibited claims

The project must not state that it has:

- reverse-engineered the exact proprietary indicator
- reproduced the original bands exactly
- discovered a physical law
- established calibrated support or resistance probabilities
- validated the model before walk-forward and baseline testing

## Allowed research language

Use:

- functional proxy
- candidate replication
- public-claim reconstruction
- structural-band hypothesis
- explanatory or predictive test

Do not use:

- exact clone
- original formula
- proven law
- guaranteed support
- certain resistance

## Research object

The true research object is not the visual indicator itself.

It is the hypothesis that:

> UTXO age/lifespan information combined with multi-horizon capital-state information can improve structural price-zone estimation beyond simple on-chain and price baselines.

## Required baseline discipline

A proxy only has value if it beats or materially complements simpler alternatives.

Therefore, no chart resemblance is considered evidence.

Evidence requires:

- frozen formulas
- point-in-time inputs
- baseline comparison
- walk-forward evaluation
- regime breakdown
- sensitivity analysis
- scorerable price or action impact

## Phase 0 verdict

Proceed to data feasibility and provenance mapping.

No framework integration is permitted at this stage.
