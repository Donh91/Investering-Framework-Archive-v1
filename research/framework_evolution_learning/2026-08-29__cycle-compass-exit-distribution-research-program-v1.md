# Cycle Compass Exit / Distribution Research Program v1

**Date:** 2026-08-29  
**Status:** ACTIVE_RESEARCH / NON_CANONICAL_MARKET_AUTHORITY  
**Purpose:** Preserve the rationale, falsification plan and prospective learning program for improving late-cycle timing without creating a new top predictor or market engine.

## Executive conclusion

The principal current weakness is not lack of late-cycle indicators. It is insufficient prospective calibration of lead time, false early exits, delayed exits, correlated-signal double counting and alt-specific opportunity cost.

The framework already has controlled language for `PARABOLIC_ALTSEASON`, `DISTRIBUTION`, `EXIT_RISK` and orthogonal warning states. Research must therefore improve timing and evidence quality around those states rather than add a competing authority layer.

## Core research questions

1. **Premature exit:** After a late-cycle warning, how much favorable upside remains at frozen 24h, 72h, 7d and later approved horizons?
2. **Late exit:** How much subsequent drawdown occurs after the same warning, and how much capital could earlier staged de-risking have preserved?
3. **Confirmation delay:** How much delay is introduced by requiring survival/persistence across ETH/BTC, breadth, BTC.D, deployment and flows, and how much false-rotation / false-distribution error does that delay remove?
4. **Warning invalidation:** How often is a `DISTRIBUTION_WARNING` or `EXIT_WARNING` later invalidated by re-acceleration, and what evidence invalidated it?
5. **Signal attribution:** Which late-cycle sensors provide unique information versus correlated restatements of euphoria, leverage or price extension?
6. **Alt-specific opportunity cost:** How much large-cap, mid-cap, small-cap and microcap upside remains after a warning, without promoting any proxy into market authority?
7. **Lead-time distribution:** What is the empirical distribution of useful warning lead time rather than a single claimed top date?

## Breadth continuity

Comparable breadth history remains a prospective evidence gap. Research should not disguise this as a code defect. T6 and any late-cycle analysis must fail closed or reduce confidence when constituent continuity / comparable history is insufficient.

The useful late-cycle question is not merely whether breadth is weak at one endpoint, but whether internal participation deteriorates persistently while headline market strength remains high.

## Adversarial / external-AI design

The correct use of a second AI is **challenger / falsifier**, not predictor authority.

For any frozen exit/distribution evidence package, a challenger should answer independently:

1. strongest case that the framework is bearish too early;
2. strongest case that it is reacting too late;
3. which 2-3 existing observations add the most incremental information;
4. which signals are likely correlated duplicates;
5. what observations at 1d / 3d / 7d / 14d would falsify the current interpretation;
6. whether the same frozen evidence maps to the same canonical phase, one phase earlier, or one phase later;
7. what evidence would justify re-acceleration and cancellation of a warning.

Challenger output has zero standalone authority and cannot alter thresholds, weights, phase or portfolio logic.

## Explicit non-goals

- No magic top indicator.
- No exact top date.
- No historical pseudo-rows created after outcomes are known.
- No automatic threshold optimization.
- No automatic portfolio execution.
- No treating extreme sentiment, high funding or parabolic price alone as distribution.
- No counting correlated signals as independent confirmations without unique-contribution evidence.

## Promotion discipline

Research may only influence later framework decisions through existing governance after sufficient prospective rows, frozen baselines, intact lineage and evidence that incremental information outweighs delay / opportunity cost.

A warning can be useful even when it does not become a phase change. Conversely, warning accuracy alone is insufficient if acting on it routinely sacrifices large remaining upside.

## Success definition

The research program succeeds when the framework can increasingly answer, with prospectively collected evidence:

> How much upside is typically left after this warning, how much downside follows if we ignore it, how much confirmation delay costs, and what evidence should cancel the warning?

That is the intended learning target for timing the transition from parabolic altseason into distribution without forcing premature exit.
