## North star

Build a persistent, reproducible historical counterfactual laboratory answering: *If the present frozen Framework had met the information available at that time, what state, warning, expected path and decision review would it have emitted; compared with cheap baselines, where is there reproducible added value, where are its failures, and which improvements transfer into future SHADOW learning?*

Three separate truth classes MUST survive every experiment:
1. ORIGINAL_ISSUED: authentic contemporaneous historical 2026 Framework forecast with original issue/delivery/source provenance.
2. FROZEN_RETROSPECTIVE: today's fixed model version replayed on period-correct knowledge, never advertised as an original 2020-2025 prediction.
3. CHALLENGER_DEVELOPMENT: model suggested or tuned after seeing historical examples, requiring an untouched chronological holdout and independent prospective follow-up.

No intervention, source, indicator, forecast, alert or portfolio-action performance may be credited to an era where its contemporaneous provider/source/availability was not proven.

1. A reproducible pilot for March 2020 market shock + matched false-alarm weeks, using only period-available source families and simple-price baseline.

## Source provenance and restrictions

Primary historic OHLC and venue-derived facts require pinned public provider version, timestamps, instrument/quote basis and full gaps. Relevant HF sources and models are metadata candidates, not automatically accepted live/PIT features. Use Historical Research Vault and existing owner source contracts. Preserve ALFRED/FRED vintage for revised macro, pre-ETF no ETF feeds, historical chain/venue availability, outcome maturity, invalidation events and source permissions. Public Git has only source-safe hashes/manifests/results; private holdings, credentials, restricted source values and raw bulk data never move into this archive.
