## The execution conveyor

A. CAPTURE_CENSUS: scan current original source owners + historical vault; choose one fixed market/event definition. Lock source hashes, timestamp/vintage semantics and rights.
B. PRE-REGISTER: freeze model commit, feature version, first-known knowledge cutoffs, target/label, matched control, cheapest baseline, horizon, primary endpoint, forbidden dates and test cohort before evaluation.
C. REPLAY: compute original-era source-legal features. Preserve UNKNOWN, missing, unissued, abstain and event-dependent samples. Distinguish 2020-2025 frozen counterfactual from admitted original 2026 evidence.
D. PARALLEL: run independent 2-4 read-only Actions research jobs where an existing original owner workflow is extended by reviewed PR; one job per independent hypothesis/data family, bounded memory/download/time and separate artifacts. Do not create cron or allow untrusted downloaded code.
E. SCORE: directional, high/low coverage+sharpness, lead-time vs false alarms, error by regime, source-quality and adjusted effective event N. Apply label maturity embargo and purged time-series cross-validation, avoiding future universe membership.
F. CHALLENGE: reproducible deterministic falsifiers first, then Grok independently critiques source time, causal attribution, false warning and alternative baselines. Sol MAX only for documented research uncertainty that deterministic work cannot resolve, and only through existing exact native authorizations.
G. DECIDE: KEEP, RETIRE, SOURCE_GAP, AWAIT_MATURITY, SHADOW_CHALLENGER, CODE_FIX or CANDIDATE_SKILL. Findings must include original-owner issue/PR and exact evidence; no direct threshold changes.
H. ACT: smallest branch/PR under owner, existing Codex intake if required; CI, independent review, qualified merge, main readback, later natural producer/consumer readback and forward shadow outcomes. S1 picks only actual authorized existing-original work, not a new autonomous queue.
I. LEARN: bind before/after source and code SHA, cohort definitions, negative findings, plausible market regime, lessons, next objective trigger. Feed discoveries into existing shadow + due-only maturation, never directly into official production rules.
J. REPEAT: advance next dependency-ready task only on authenticated new receipt, source changed, maturity event, CI/merge or explicit observed blocker resolution. If no eligible work, terminate quiet, do not spin.

## Core research families and falsifiers

- Crisis vulnerability: test genuinely pre-onset risk indicators plus crisis-family negative control for March 2020, 2022 contagion, March 2023 and Oct 2025; separate unknowable headline surprise from detectable fragility.
- Pullback or systemic transition: hard negative controls from severe non-crash corrections; score escalation 3/5 vs suggested 4/5 with proper false positives, NOT sell authority.
- Altseason ignition and exit: ETH/BTC persistence, breadth survival, BTC dominance with stable denominator, liquidity deployment and rotation by tier; require source-era tradable full universe; no retro winner cherry-picking.
- Recovery and bull traps: classify first rebound, genuine reclaim, retest and missed upside; net after cost, against HOLD and WAIT.
- Weekly CN: one prospectively frozen 7-day BTC/ETH close + low/high per day each historical Monday; status distinguish MODEL_REPLAY from original first-issued. The original W45 Nov 2 BTC/ETH 0/7 is NOT repaired by counterfactual replays.
- News + macro: issue/source first-known, publisher and collector clocks, FRED ALFRED vintages, duplicated narratives and embargo; LLM may classify source, not infer unpublished news.
- Paper economics: whole-position sell capacity, slippage, fees/gas, no fill, time-to-delivery, tax unknown, paper-only, sensitivity to late warnings and re-entry.
- Source/architecture shock: change source family, ETF-era availability, venue gaps and historically absent candles; must abstain rather than substitute a modern synthetic feature.
